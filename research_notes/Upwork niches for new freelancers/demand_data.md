> Method note (research date 2026-10-24): **Every WebFetch call failed with DNS errors** (upwork.com, gigradar.io, tryvibeworker.com, upwatcher.io), so **all findings come from search-engine snippets and summaries**, not full-page reads. Spot-check the numbers before quoting them. This file extends `../Upwork freelancer success playbook/market_trends.md`. It does not repeat that file's company metrics (Q1/Q2 2026 GSV, client counts, take rate, guidance cut) or its In-Demand Skills headline numbers (AI +109%, AI video +329%, AI integration +178%, annotation +154%, chatbots +71%, ecommerce mgmt +130%, medical VA +44%). Source types are labeled as follows: **[Upwork]** means company-published, **[Vendor]** means a tool seller with a commercial interest (GigRadar, Vibeworker, SnipeWork, JobsPipe), and **[Blog]** means an affiliate or SEO guide.

## 1. Where demand is: volume vs. growth (Upwork data plus 2026 posting samples)

### Takeaway
Upwork's growth headlines are about AI skills, but **AI-tagged posts are only about 3% of volume**. In raw posting counts, the largest pools are still **Design & Creative (graphic design, video editing), Sales & Marketing (social media marketing), and Web/Shopify dev**. Upwork says AI-related GSV grew 22% and AI Strategy & Consulting more than 50% in Q2 2026, while AI automation is eating "very low-complexity" work.

### Cited Findings
**Upwork's own statements (official)**
- Q2 2026: AI-related GSV was up more than 22% year over year to about $330M run-rate, and the **AI Strategy & Consulting** subcategory grew more than 50% (51% per call coverage). Management said the 22% figure understates AI work because clients often stop naming AI in their posts. [Upwork] — [Upwork 8-K Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001627475/000162747526000046/upwork2q26-pressrelease.htm); [Yahoo/GuruFocus call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/upwork-inc-upwk-q2-2026-050206592.html)
- Management said AI automation accelerated in Q2 2026, **mainly in very low-complexity work**. [Upwork, via transcript] — [Equibles transcript](https://equibles.com/stocks/upwk/calls/2026-q2)
- On the call, management cited growing client demand to "humanize" AI translation output. [Upwork, via transcript] — [Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-upwork-tops-q2-2026-eps-estimates-but-stock-sinks-on-outlook-93CH-4850502)
- In-Demand Skills 2026: AI-referencing skills grew 109%, versus **23% growth for other in-demand skills**. Full-stack dev, general VA, data analytics and graphic design were the most consistently in-demand by volume. A secondary list adds AI image generation/editing at +95% (not confirmed against the primary source). [Upwork, via secondary] — [Quartz](https://qz.com/demand-is-rising-for-these-ai-skills-in-2026); [Upwork IR PDF](https://investors.upwork.com/node/12681/pdf); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/09/upwork-fastest-growing-in-demand-skills-companies-are-hiring-for.html); [Monitask](https://www.monitask.com/blog/upwork-top-most-wanted-skills/)
- The earlier 2025 report showed AI skills such as GenAI modeling and annotation growing up to 220% year over year, with advanced-AI freelancers earning up to 22% more per hour. [Upwork, via secondary] — [Small Business Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/upwork-most-in-demand-skills-2025/)

**Posting-volume samples (vendors, independent of each other)**
- Vibeworker analyzed **252,697 skill-tagged posts from Jun 1 to Jul 31, 2026**. Top skills: **Graphic Design 32,887 posts (12.9% of the July feed)**, **Video Editing 24,017**, **Social Media Marketing 22,693**, then Web Design and Photoshop. Among development tags: JavaScript 10,512, **Shopify 8,878**, Python 6,144, React 4,069. **All AI-tagged skills together were about 3% of postings.** The dataset does not measure proposals or hires. [Vendor] — [Vibeworker skills analysis](https://tryvibeworker.com/blog/most-in-demand-upwork-skills)
- SnipeWork's weekly index for September 2026 has **Design & Creative** as the top category every week. In week 37 the counts were Design & Creative 4,098, Sales & Marketing 3,421, and Web/Mobile/Software Dev 2,716. The share columns in the snippets were garbled. [Vendor] — [SnipeWork W37](https://snipework.com/market/index/2026-W37); [W36](https://snipework.com/market/index/2026-W36)
- GigRadar March 2026: **Artificial Intelligence** was the fastest-growing category at +18.1% month over month (2,654 jobs), but with only a 3.8% reply rate ("supply has flooded ahead of demand"). Web/Mobile/Software Dev was the busiest category, at a 4.0% reply rate. A June 2026 update says the AI Integration & Automation sub-segment grew more than 90%. [Vendor] — [GigRadar March 2026](https://gigradar.io/upwork/reports/2026/03/all-industries); [GigRadar market report 2026](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-market-report-2026)
- GigRadar Q2 2026 counted **448,118 jobs posted in Q2, down 14.2% quarter over quarter**. Fixed-price jobs over $5K fell 18.8%, while jobs under $100 fell only 12.2% and gained share. **Expert-level jobs fell 19.1%, against 10.9% for entry-level jobs.** [Vendor] — [GigRadar Q2 2026](https://gigradar.io/upwork/reports/2026/q2/all-industries)
- Vibeworker reported July 2026 postings up 26% to 161,102, from 127,607 in June. This **conflicts in direction** with GigRadar's Q2 decline, though the periods and methods differ. [Vendor] — [Vibeworker July 2026](https://tryvibeworker.com/blog/upwork-job-market-report-july-2026)

### Inferences
- "Fastest-growing" and "biggest" are different lists. AI niches are growing from a small base (about 3% of posts), while design, video, social media and Shopify dominate absolute volume. A beginner needs many small jobs to bid on, which favors high-volume niches, or AI-adjacent sub-niches inside them (AI video editing, AI-assisted design, Shopify plus AI automation).
- Entry-level jobs held up better than expert jobs in Q2, a relative bright spot for newcomers. In absolute terms, though, entry-level posts are only about 8–11% of the feed (see section 2).

### Gaps
- I could not get Upwork's official per-category GSV trend (writing, translation, design and so on). The company discloses only AI-related GSV and AI Strategy & Consulting.
- I could not fetch the full In-Demand Skills 2026 list beyond the headline skills. Marketing, writing and design sub-skills were not visible in snippets.

## 2. Competition and entry barriers by niche (where newcomers can win)

### Takeaway
The best vendor data available measures **proposal reply rates by category**, which serves as a proxy for competition. It shows the "glamour" categories (Web, Mobile, AI/ML) as the most crowded, at about 4–7% reply. "Under-fished" categories such as lead gen/telemarketing, game design, sales copywriting, PR/brand strategy, infosec and product design reply at about 11–15%, and engineering at about 10%. Writing deteriorated most in Q2 2026. Only about 1 in 12 posts is labeled entry level.

### Cited Findings
- GigRadar analyzed 133,872 outbound proposals sent through its tool between Dec 2025 and Feb 2026. The platform mean reply rate was **7.45%**. Web Development **5.80%**, Mobile **5.60%**, AI & ML **7.21%**. The "under-fished six" replied at **10.78–14.58%**: Game Design, Lead Gen & Telemarketing, Sales & Marketing Copywriting, Marketing PR & Brand Strategy, Information Security, and Product Design. Cost per reply varies 6.4x across categories, with Web/Mobile/Software Dev at about $34 per reply in Connects. [Vendor; mainly agency proposals, not a random sample] — [GigRadar category map](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-job-categories-agencies)
- GigRadar Q2 2026: the overall reply rate fell from 5.8% to **5.1%** and the view rate from 18.3% to 17.7%. Engineering replies at **10.3%** and telemarketing at **16.1%**. **Writing had the largest view-rate drop** (−6.1 pts to 25.6%), and its reply rate fell 4.2 pts to **5.2%**. A first reply costs about **$14 in Connects for video editing versus $50 for web development**. [Vendor] — [GigRadar Q2 2026](https://gigradar.io/upwork/reports/2026/q2/all-industries)
- GigRadar on video: Jan–Feb 2026 proposals targeting "video/animation/motion" replied at **10.76%**, against a 6.95% baseline. [Vendor] — [GigRadar video editing](https://gigradar.io/blog/freelance-video-editing)
- **Entry-level share of posts:** SnipeWork's September 2026 weekly figures were 8%, 9% and 8% entry level, with about 60–64% intermediate and 28–31% expert, and 11% in the week of Sep 28–Oct 4. Vibeworker's June 2026 data has about 1 in 12 posts labeled Entry level and about two-thirds Intermediate. [Vendor] — [SnipeWork W36](https://snipework.com/market/index/2026-W36); [W38](https://snipework.com/market/index/2026-W38); [W40](https://snipework.com/market/index/2026-W40); [Vibeworker June 2026](https://tryvibeworker.com/blog/upwork-job-market-report-june-2026)
- JobsPipe's May 2026 sample of 5,001 live listings: **82% payment-verified**, a $100 median fixed budget and a $12/hr median starting hourly rate. The sample is US-led and weighted toward creative and marketing work. [Vendor] — [JobsPipe Upwork statistics 2026](https://jobspipe.dev/blog/upwork-statistics-2026)
- Beginner guides commonly recommend data entry/research ($12–20/hr), social media scheduling, short copy, WordPress fixes ($20–40/hr), transcription/captioning, VA and LinkedIn outreach. They advise narrow positioning (e.g. "Shopify speed optimization for DTC brands") and targeting posts with 5–15 proposals rather than 50+. They also note many users call Upwork "oversaturated" in 2026. [Blog; no measured competition data] — [EduEarnHub](https://eduearnhub.com/?p=5509); [GetMany beginner tips](https://getmany.com/blog/upwork-tips-for-beginners); [SideHustles](https://sidehustles.com/is-upwork-worth-it/)
- Data annotation entry points: some Upwork annotation posts explicitly welcome careful newcomers and provide training. Others require prior experience on Outlier, Alignerr or Mercor, or C1/C2 English. [Upwork job listings, via snippets] — [EU annotators post](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Based-Data-Annotators-for-Projects_~022104920490392355971/); [AI trainer post](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Trainer-Data-Annotation-Specialist-Generalist-Reasoning-Tasks_~022095097768852308919/); [$100 fixed annotation post](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Data-Annotation-Specialist-Needed-and-Computer-Vision-NLP-RLHF-Training-Data_~022079340383009161860/)

### Inferences
- Lower competition, measured by reply rate, shows up in **sales-side and less fashionable work**: lead gen/telemarketing, sales copy, PR/brand, plus video and engineering. Higher competition is in **web dev, mobile, generic AI/ML and writing**. A newcomer with no badge will find it easiest to get replies in under-fished niches and in video.
- Because only about 8–11% of posts are labeled entry level, a newcomer has to bid on intermediate posts. Small fixed-price posts under $100 are the realistic first-job pool (see section 4).
- The GigRadar numbers reflect agencies using a paid bidding tool. Reply rates for a no-history individual are probably lower across the board. The *relative* ranking is the useful part.

### Gaps
- I found no published proposals-per-job count by category for 2026, and no hire-rate or time-to-hire figures by category.
- I found no data at all on **how long newcomers take to land a first job**, by niche or overall. Any "weeks to first job" figure would be anecdotal.
- I found no GigRadar Q2 figures for data entry, admin/VA or design (snippets did not include them).

## 3. AI's effect on each niche (shrinking vs. growing)

### Takeaway
Growing: AI integration/automation, AI strategy/consulting, AI video, annotation/AI training, chatbots, ecommerce management, and "humanizing" AI output (translation post-editing, cleanup of vibe-coded apps). Shrinking or under pressure: writing (worst Q2 drop in engagement), translation, simple graphics, and other low-complexity tasks. The prior file covers the academic evidence on writing, image work and translation rates.

### Cited Findings
- **Growing (Upwork official):** AI Strategy & Consulting grew more than 50% in Q2 2026, and AI-related GSV grew more than 22%. — [Upwork 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001627475/000162747526000046/upwork2q26-pressrelease.htm)
- **Shrinking (Upwork official):** automation is accelerating "mainly in very low-complexity work". — [Equibles transcript](https://equibles.com/stocks/upwk/calls/2026-q2)
- **Writing:** largest view-rate drop of any category in Q2 2026, and reply rate down to 5.2%. [Vendor] — [GigRadar Q2 2026](https://gigradar.io/upwork/reports/2026/q2/all-industries)
- **Translation is shifting to post-editing:** clients increasingly ask to "humanize" AI translation. — [Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-upwork-tops-q2-2026-eps-estimates-but-stock-sinks-on-outlook-93CH-4850502)
- **Vibe-code cleanup** is an emerging niche. 404 Media reports, via Mezha, that freelancers and firms are making money fixing AI-written code; one Fiverr provider handles 15–20 such clients a year. Claimed freelance rates are $100–300/hr (Metana, a bootcamp vendor), while a US agency list puts agency rates at $25–99/hr (Litslink, an agency vendor). Security-flaw statistics are inconsistent between sources (about 45% vs. 65%). **I found no Upwork-specific job counts.** — [Mezha (citing 404 Media)](https://mezha.ua/en/news/freelancers-and-companies-making-money-fixing-vibe-code-304768/); [Metana](https://metana.io/blog/what-is-vibe-coding-cleanup/); [Litslink](https://litslink.com/blog/top-vibe-coding-cleanup-service-companies-in-the-us); [example Fiverr gig](https://fiverr.com/jarenlem/build-fix-vibe-coded-app-website)
- **No-code AI automation (n8n/Make/Zapier):** blog-reported Upwork rates are n8n $40–100/hr (senior $125–250+), Make about $60–120/hr and Zapier about $45–85/hr. Zapier is described as the easiest entry and quickest to convert SMB clients; n8n self-hosting requires Docker/VPS skills. Retainers are cited at about $1.2K–3.5K a month per client. These are blog/vendor estimates, and the earnings claims are anecdotal. [Blog] — [AdSnipper n8n](https://adsnipper.com/blog/hire-n8n-developer/); [BetOnAI](https://betonai.net/how-to-build-3k-15k-month-ai-automation-business-2026-n8n-make-zapier-fiverr-upwork-retainers/); [Hackceleration](https://hackceleration.com/labs/best/automation-platforms-freelancers)

### Inferences
- The safest newcomer bets combine an **AI-growth tailwind** with a **small-job format**: AI video editing/shorts, Zapier/Make/n8n automations for SMBs, chatbot setup, data annotation/AI training tasks, Shopify/ecommerce management, and fixing small bugs in vibe-coded apps. Note that "AI & ML" as a broad label is crowded (3.8–7.2% reply), so positioning on specific tools and outcomes matters.
- Writing, translation, basic logo design and generic VA work face the double hit of AI substitution and heavy supply. They are poor first niches unless reframed as AI-output editing or humanizing, or as a specialist VA role (medical VA +44%, ecommerce management +130%, per the prior file).

### Gaps
- I found no Upwork-published decline percentages for writing, translation or design categories in 2025–2026.
- I found no Upwork job-volume data specific to vibe-code cleanup or n8n.

## 4. Typical pay by niche (2026) and the small-budget reality

### Takeaway
Upwork's own guide gives bands rather than medians: about $10–25/hr for entry-level/admin and $75–150+/hr for specialized dev, AI and consulting. Posted fixed budgets are small: a $80–100 median, about 42% under $50 in July 2026, and an estimated effective rate of about $6–9/hr. Newcomers should expect small first jobs.

### Cited Findings
- Upwork rate bands: entry-level and admin roles at $10–25/hr; specialized dev, AI and consulting often at $75–150+/hr. [Upwork] — [Upwork hourly rates guide](https://www.upwork.com/resources/upwork-hourly-rates)
- Jobbers 2026 global medians, self-described estimates and not Upwork-specific: specialized consultants $125/hr, business consultants $110, data science/ML $95, software developers $85, UI/UX $75. [Blog/vendor] — [Jobbers rate index 2026](https://www.jobbers.io/the-global-freelance-hourly-rate-index-2026/)
- Clockify averages, platform unclear: programmers about $38/hr, web developers $32.50, project managers $32, writers/editors $27.50, graphic designers $25, bookkeepers $18. North American cross-industry median about $95/hr versus about $25/hr for South and Southeast Asia. [Vendor] — [Clockify](https://clockify.me/average-hourly-rates); [GigRadar hourly rate](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-hourly-rate)
- Vibeworker July 2026: 77,696 fixed-price and 55,162 hourly posts. **Median fixed budget $80** (down from $100 in June). **About 42% of fixed posts were under $50** (up from 31.5%), and only **17.4% were $500 or more** (down from 22.3%). The estimated effective median was **about $6.25/hr** (down from $8.75), calculated from budget divided by LLM-estimated hours. [Vendor] — [Vibeworker July 2026](https://tryvibeworker.com/blog/upwork-job-market-report-july-2026)
- Data annotation on Upwork pays about $15–50/hr according to CareerSeeker. Mercor cites $15–60+/hr for AI trainer and RLHF roles, but Mercor is an AI-training platform marketing itself. [Blog/vendor] — [CareerSeeker](https://careerseeker.ai/data-annotation-jobs/); [Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/resources/experts/best-entry-level-ai-jobs-guide/)
- The prior file already covers the effect of Upwork's 0–15% variable fee (about 10% typical) and Connects costs on net pay.

### Inferences
- Indicative pay ranking, high to low (my synthesis, not measured data): AI integration/automation and consulting; software dev; cleanup of vibe-coded apps; data/ML; UI/UX and product design; video editing; Shopify/ecommerce; marketing and lead gen; annotation; design and writing; data entry/VA. For a newcomer, *first-job* pay will sit well below these ranges. The small-budget pool (under $100) is where first reviews usually come from.

### Gaps
- I found no Upwork-published median hourly rate per category for 2026.
- I found no data linking niche to time-to-first-job or newcomer win rate.

## 5. Nigerian/African freelancers: which niches work

### Takeaway
I found **no Upwork-published or independent dataset** on which categories Nigerian or African freelancers succeed in. Secondary and anecdotal sources point to software engineering, AI training/data annotation, copywriting/content, design, video editing, data entry and VA. Nigeria's platform freelancing grew about 130% over five years, as the prior file notes.

### Cited Findings
- A 2026 industry piece names software engineering and AI training/data annotation as Nigeria's strengths, with no Upwork-specific figures. Nigeria's ICT sector was 11.31% of real GDP in Q1 2026, up from 10.59%. [Secondary, via snippet] — [Worksuite top countries](https://worksuite.com/resources/insights/top-countries-to-hire-freelancers)
- TechCabal (July 2025) profiled African freelancers. Nigerians in copywriting and content marketing; one copywriter gets most of his work through Upwork. Kenyans in SEO and Web3 content. [Press, anecdotal] — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/07/24/10-african-freelancers-on-their-tools-of-productivity/)
- A 2024 Bookipi study of Upwork pricing ranked Nigeria 23rd and Kenya 32nd on average freelancer pay, across logo design, video editing, data entry and VA. The data is dated. — [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/news/nigeria-ranks-among-nations-with-high-pay-for-freelancers/); [Tech-ish](https://tech-ish.com/2024/04/17/report-kenya-freelancers-9th-globally/)
- Nigeria-focused guides claim rates of $25–150/hr, or $60–150 for experienced web developers. These are unverified marketing figures from course and platform sellers. [Blog] — [Jobbers Nigeria](https://www.jobbers.io/?p=53015); [MyJobMag](https://www.myjobmag.com/blog/best-freelance-skills-you-need-to-earn-in-usd)
- Some annotation posts restrict by region (e.g. "EU-based"), and some require near-native English. — [Upwork EU annotators post](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Based-Data-Annotators-for-Projects_~022104920490392355971/)

### Inferences
- With South/Southeast Asia rates around $25/hr median and US clients dominating demand, a Nigerian newcomer can price competitively in the $10–25/hr band. Price alone does not set them apart, though: Philippines, India and Pakistan supply is heavy in VA, data entry and design.
- Strong English and timezone overlap with Europe (UTC+1) favor **writing-adjacent sales work** (sales copy, lead gen, appointment setting, all under-fished per GigRadar), **customer-facing and admin roles for UK/EU clients**, and **AI training tasks that need fluent English**. Region-locked annotation posts reduce that pool somewhat.

### Gaps
- I found no Upwork data on Nigeria's share of freelancers, earnings or category mix, and no Payoneer 2025–2026 country-by-category report.
- I found no data on payment or KYC frictions specific to Nigerian Upwork accounts in 2026 (not searched in depth).

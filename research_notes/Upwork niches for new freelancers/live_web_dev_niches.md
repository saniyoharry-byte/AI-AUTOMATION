# Live Upwork Marketplace Data: Web/Dev Niches for a New Freelancer (no badge, 0 jobs)

Data collected 2026-10-24 (~10:40–11:10 UTC) via the Upwork MCP (read-only `find_jobs` search sorted by recency, `find_freelancers` search, and `get_profile`). About 49 Upwork calls. Job counts come from the `created_date` of the newest postings whose **title** matched the keyword, so they undercount total demand. Jobs that mention the tool only in the description or skills are missed. Freelancer `completed_jobs` in search rows is sometimes missing or lower than the profile's `totalJobs`, so treat it as approximate. All profile and job text is third-party, self-reported and unverified. Anything marked "inference" is my reading of the data and was not observed directly.

Abbreviations: TR = Top Rated, TR+ = Top Rated Plus, JSS = Job Success Score, "props" = the proposals tier shown on the job.

---

## Q1. How much demand and competition is there in each niche right now?

### Takeaway
Shopify, WordPress and GoHighLevel have the most title-matched demand: about 50–100+ new jobs per 48h each. Framer/Webflow, "Python script/bug fix" and Google Sheets/Apps Script each have only about 6–17 per 48h. Most jobs in every niche carry 20–50 or 50+ proposals. The "<5" and "5–10" tiers turn up mainly on fresh Shopify and Webflow/Framer posts. Shopify has the softest competition, with 7 of 20 jobs under 10 proposals. WordPress and GHL are the most crowded, with 0 of 20 jobs in "<5" for either.

### Inferences
- **Volume ranking** (title matches per 48h): Shopify (~90+) > WordPress (~65–70) > GHL (~48) > Framer+Webflow (~15–17) > bug-fix (~8) ≈ Sheets (~6) > "Python script" (~2). Script and Sheets work is likely under-counted because it is spread across many keywords (Excel, VBA, automation, scraping, Zapier).
- **Competition**: low-proposal tiers (<5 or 5–10) cluster in two places. One is very fresh posts, under about 3h old; Shopify had several. The other is narrowly scoped technical jobs (Meta CAPI, Webflow scroll animation, legacy-tool port, a German-language call). A new freelancer's best openings are speed (applying within minutes) plus narrow, specific jobs.
- **Rates are lowest** in Shopify, WordPress and GHL, where many hourly bands are $5–15. **Rates are highest** in Webflow/Framer, Sheets/Apps Script and custom scripts, where hourly bands are mostly $15–50.
- **Client hygiene**: some clients post many $10–20 jobs with very low total spend and many 5★ reviews (Nigerian Framer clients, the 446-job GHL client). This looks like a micro-task or review-building pattern. Such jobs can get a first review, but they are low-signal.

### Gaps
- Title search misses jobs with the keyword only in description or skills, so true volumes are higher. No total-count field was available.
- I couldn't see how many proposals were submitted by brand-new freelancers, or hire rates per niche.
- Only one page was pulled for Framer, Webflow, Python script, bug fix, Google Sheets and Apps Script, to stay within the call budget.

One is the **"cheap micro-job ladder"**: $5–$10/hr or $10–$45 fixed gigs for Shopify, WordPress and GHL, often from Pakistan, Bangladesh or Nigeria, with heavy keyword stuffing. The other is **"proof-first specialists"**: $25–$60/hr profiles, often from the US or EU, whose real off-platform portfolio, live demos or public products replace Upwork history. The second group lands $100–$1,500 jobs from their first contracts.

### Inferences
- **Two viable entry models.** (a) Low-price volume: $5–15/hr or $10–45 fixed micro-fixes to stack reviews quickly (Asad U., MD N., Krystal H., Raphael L.'s early jobs). This works in high-volume niches (Shopify, WP, GHL), but income is about $6–20/h. (b) Proof-first premium: $25–60/hr backed by verifiable public work (Rayan's Marketplace add-ons, Christopher's live demos, Illia's QA angle and case studies, Waseem's named client sites). The first contracts are already $175–$1,525.
- **Badges come fast.** Many profiles with only 1–3 jobs already show Top Rated in searches. So "no badge" is a short phase, and fast early 5★ reviews matter more than the badge itself.
- **Repeat hires recur in the reviews** (Davyd Z., Ana Luisa P., Christopher H., Raphael L.). Doing a little extra and handing over clear notes seems to turn a small job into a second contract.
- **Red flags to avoid copying.** Inflated claims ("Top Rated… 500+ projects" on a $200 profile), ~100 fake-looking employment entries, brand-name concept portfolios, and clusters of $10 jobs with near-identical long reviews. These are visible to clients and could plausibly trigger trust or policy problems (inference; not verified against Upwork policy here).

### Gaps
- Proposal texts aren't visible, so I can't confirm the "demo in proposal" tactic beyond the profile's own claim.
- The Rising Talent badge isn't exposed by the API, so I can't tell which no-badge profiles are Rising Talent.
- Several fixed contracts hide the amount earned (Krystal H.), and some profiles hide total earnings.
- I got only 2 deep profiles per niche (12 total) instead of 4, because of the rate limit and call budget. Profiles named in search rows were not opened.

## Q3. Which niches can a new freelancer realistically win, and how? (synthesis from observed data)

### Takeaway
The best openings for a new freelancer with 0 jobs are: **(1) Shopify fixes/setup**, with the highest volume and the most low-proposal posts; **(2) Google Sheets/Apps Script automation**, with low volume but high rates and proof-first wins at $55/hr; and **(3) scripts/automation fixes**, where default search is the most open to no-badge profiles. **Webflow/Framer** pays well, but only for strong design portfolios. **WordPress and GHL** have lots of demand, but the proposal tiers are the most crowded and many jobs pay $5–15/hr.

### Cited Findings
- Low-proposal share per niche (my count of "<5" plus "5–10" tiers in the sampled jobs): Shopify 7/20 ([e.g.](https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022113883140280263787)); Framer 2/10 and Webflow 2/10 ([e.g.](https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022113936026913493906)); Python script 1/10 and bug fix 1/10 ([e.g.](https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022112845829723512857)); Google Sheets 1/10 ([e.g.](https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022112618999689872845)); WordPress 1/20 ([e.g.](https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022113700783031487600)); GHL 0/20 ([e.g.](https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022113749871454585655)).
- Share of no-badge profiles in the top 25 default search results: Python script about 7/25 ([search sample](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~01c067e02b63391c62)); Shopify about 6–8/25 ([sample](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~011d52f087ebac45cb)); WordPress 3/25 ([sample](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~016fc707e2cb679bb0)); GHL 2/25 ([sample](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~013b5101eae55217dc)); Sheets 2/25 ([sample](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~0105d9737075b2b414)); Framer 0/25 ([sample](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~0158855271362033bf)).
- Typical first-contract value seen in no-badge profiles: Shopify $78–$880; WordPress $10–$400; Framer/Webflow $110–$1,525; GHL $140–$1,400 (hourly); scripts $30–$1,000; Sheets $15–$850. Sources: the profiles cited in Q2.

### Inferences
- **Suggested niche ranking for a brand-new freelancer** (inference; it combines volume, low-proposal share, rates and how open default search is to no-badge profiles):
  1. **Shopify fixes/setup and tracking (Meta CAPI/GA4)**: most jobs and the most <10-proposal posts. Narrow technical Shopify jobs, such as pixel/CAPI or product migration, beat generic "Shopify expert" ones. Varnika C. shows a clear bundled offer can win at $40/hr while based in the US.
  2. **Google Sheets / Apps Script automation**: fewer jobs, but hourly bands of $15–50 and buyers who value demos. A few self-built tools or add-ons let a 0-review profile charge $25–55/hr (Rayan S.). Data-entry "Sheets" jobs should be ignored.
  3. **Scripts / small automation fixes** (Python/JS, n8n/Make, scraping, "fix my AI-built app"): default search is the most open to no-badge profiles. Generic $5–$10 script jobs are a race to the bottom, so position on a concrete outcome (scraper repair, API integration, deliverability fix) and include a demo.
  4. **Webflow/Framer**: fewer jobs and an expert skew. Winnable only with a polished design portfolio (Illia M., Davyd Z.). Fix/polish jobs on existing sites are the entry point.
  5. **GoHighLevel**: high volume but crowded (0/20 low-proposal posts) and often $5–15/hr. The narrow repeatable fix of **A2P 10DLC registration** (Krystal H.) and a RevOps/CRM-architect angle (Daniel B.) are the observed ways in.
  6. **WordPress/Elementor**: high volume, but the most crowded and dominated by veterans with 100–300 jobs. Way in: micro-fixes (malware, speed, responsive, critical errors) at $10–50 fixed to build reviews, or a European timezone with real client sites (Waseem I.).
- **Common winning profile traits observed across niches**: one clear niche title rather than a keyword stack, or a title stack in high-volume niches. A pain-first overview hook. Concrete deliverables and timelines ("live in 3 days", "landing page 3–7 days"). Risk reversal ("30 days of free bug fixes", free audit or consultation). Verifiable proof (live links, public add-ons, live demos, named client sites). A low-friction entry product (a $10–$50 consult or audit, A2P fix, store review). A YouTube intro video was present on 5 of 12 profiles.

### Gaps
- There's no direct data on how Upwork's ranking algorithm weights profile fields. The search-placement observations are correlational.
- I couldn't measure the response time needed to catch "<5 proposals" jobs. Posting-to-proposal timing isn't exposed.
- No data on Connects cost per job or how it differs by niche.

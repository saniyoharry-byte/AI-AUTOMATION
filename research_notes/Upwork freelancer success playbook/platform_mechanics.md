## 1. Search ranking, Best Match, Uma matching and invites

### Takeaway
Upwork confirms that matching relies on profile data (skills, categories, services), the client's stated job requirements, and history/quality signals (JSS, earnings, badges). It does not publish weights. Since Oct 2025, Uma Recruiter, an AI agent, builds client shortlists automatically, and since May 2026 it is available on the free Basic client plan. A new freelancer's first visibility therefore depends heavily on how well the profile text matches the job, and on recent activity.

### Cited Findings
- An Upwork community moderator said job-feed Best Match uses "a combination of factors," most "aligned with the requirements selected and mentioned by the client while posting a job." Another Upwork answer said the factors can't be disclosed. — [Community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Freelancers/Best-Matched-Algorithm-for-quot-Recently-posted-quot-Jobs/m-p/1521926); [Community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Freelancers/Best-Matches-Job-Feed/m-p/1211125)
- Upwork product updates say Best Match enhancements show which freelancers' skills best meet client needs. Talent search results display JSS, total earnings and Top Rated status (Spring 2024, older). — [Upwork Spring 2024 updates](https://www.upwork.com/updates/spring-2024); [Upwork resource](https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-upwork-search-feature-helps-clients-freelancers-quickly-connect)
- Upwork's help center says matching relies heavily on users' information (skills, categories, services). Already in prior notes. — [Intro to Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/34954643219987-Intro-to-Upwork)
- **Uma Recruiter:** initially launched October 2025, per Upwork's engineering blog. — [Upwork blog](https://www.upwork.com/blog/uma-recruiter-how-we-built-an-agentic-solution-to-talent-matching-and-hiring)
- The Spring 2026 update (May 5, 2026) brought Uma shortlisting to the Basic (free) client plan. Upwork says it "automatically identifies and surfaces the most relevant professionals for a project." Shortlists arrive in about 6 hours. Upwork says jobs filled within 7 days rose 10% between Nov 2025 and Mar 2026. — [Investor release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-updates-spring-2026-ai-powered-innovations-help-small); [Upwork blog Spring 2026](https://www.upwork.com/blog/updates-spring-2026); [StockTitan copy](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/UPWK/upwork-updates-spring-2026-ai-powered-innovations-to-help-small-hwxlroim0a8a.html)
- Summer 2025: Upwork said Uma was evolving into an "always-on work agent," powered a majority of new client job posts, and raised successful matches on high-value projects by 8%. Uma also runs interviews in the proposal process and gives clients structured summaries. — [Investor release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-evolves-uma-ai-ai-work-agent-advances-human-ai)
- Upwork's 2026 hiring guide says Uma helps clients go "from shortlisting quality matches to interviewing candidates." — [Upwork hiring guide](https://upwork.com/resources/how-to-hire-freelancers)
- [3P] Uma matches job posts against freelancer profiles and activity signals and recommends a shortlist. It can invite freelancers on the client's behalf, including on Basic plans. — [Snipework](https://snipework.com/blog/upwork-uma-recruiter-guide). **Not confirmed by any Upwork source found.**
- [3P] Claims that are not confirmed by Upwork:
  - Clients opt in to shortlisting when posting.
  - Shortlisting is unavailable for private and legal-category jobs.
  - When a client enables it, an Uma instant interview is required before a freelancer can submit a proposal.
  - Uma shortlisting gave 30% more hires and 11% faster time-to-hire in 2025 tests.
  — [UpHunt](https://uphunt.io/blog/upwork-uma-recruiter-ai-shortlist-freelancer-guide-2026); [UpAlerts](https://upalerts.app/blog/what-is-upwork-uma)
- [3P] Guides list relevance to search terms, past performance, activity level, JSS and availability/response rate as ranking factors. Their percentage weights are guesses. — [GigUp](https://giguphq.com/blog/upwork-search-algorithm-2026); [Jobbers](https://www.jobbers.io/?p=80205)
- Boosted Profile (paid with Connects) places you among the top profiles when clients are prompted to invite freelancers. Already in prior notes. — [Upwork ads guide](https://www.upwork.com/guides/ads)

### Inferences
- Invites now come from three channels:
  - Client manual search (ranked by relevance and history signals)
  - Uma auto-shortlists (relevance to the job post)
  - Paid Boosted Profile placement
- With 0 jobs, the realistic levers are exact skill/keyword match (Roblox, Luau, Roblox Studio, game type), a complete profile, being recently active, and keeping availability current.
- Because Uma reads the job post against the profile, a profile written in client language ("Roblox simulator/tycoon scripting, DataStores, monetization") likely matters more than generic "game developer" wording.

### Gaps
- No official Upwork list or weighting of Best Match or Uma signals. Whether location, response time or hourly rate affect ranking is unconfirmed.
- I couldn't verify from Upwork whether Uma sends invites automatically or gates proposals with AI interviews.

## 2. Badges and Job Success Score

### Takeaway
- **Rising Talent** is the only badge a 0-job freelancer can realistically target. Upwork's current wording bases it on a complete profile, prior experience, proposal quality and recent activity, with no JSS required.
- **JSS** is computed daily from 6-, 12- and 24-month contract windows and appears only after enough contract history.
- **Top Rated Plus** requires large contracts.
- **Expert-Vetted** is invitation-only (top 1%).

### Cited Findings
- **Rising Talent (current Upwork wording)** — [Help 360049702614](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049702614); [Talent badges explained](https://www.upwork.com/resources/talent-badges-explained)
  - 100% complete profile with signs of strong experience before joining Upwork
  - Positive performance on Upwork, such as high-quality proposals and on-time delivery
  - Up-to-date availability
  - Activity in the past 90 days, or joined in the last 30 days
  - Follows the ToS
  - The comparison table lists "No JSS" for Rising Talent
  - Specialty, listed skills, education and pre-Upwork experience are also considered
- **Rising Talent conflict:**
  - Prior notes (help 211063228) and [3P] UpHunt cite a different version: Upwork invitation, or ≥4.8 rating plus ≥$250 earned in 12 months. — [UpHunt](https://uphunt.io/blog/upwork-rising-talent-badge-2026)
  - The two versions conflict. Help article 360049702614 appears to be the newer wording, but I couldn't fetch either page to compare dates.
- **Top Rated Plus** — [Community announcement](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Announcements/New-talent-badges-are-now-available/m-p/778379); [Upwork help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063568-Top-Rated-and-Rising-Talent)
  - Has all Top Rated criteria plus a history of large contracts.
  - The large-contract threshold varies by category: $20K in some, $30K in Web, Mobile & Software Dev (older community post).
- **Top Rated earnings conflict:**
  - A snippet from "Talent badges explained" says all Top Rated freelancers earned >$10,000 in the past 12 months. — [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/resources/talent-badges-explained)
  - The help article cited in prior notes says ≥$1,000. — [Help 211068468](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211068468)
  - Unresolved; the $10K figure may refer to Top Rated Plus.
- **Expert-Vetted** — [Help 360049702614](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049702614); [Community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Announcements/New-talent-badges-are-now-available/m-p/778379); [3P Raenest](https://www.raenest.com/blog/upwork-badges)
  - Invitation only.
  - Screening by Talent Managers may include portfolio review, coding tests and interviews.
  - Requires exceptional expertise, strong communication, and being in the top 1% of talent.
  - Limited categories: dev, design/creative, sales/marketing.
  - Reportedly highlighted mainly to Business Plus/Enterprise clients.
- **JSS** — [What is a Job Success Score](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/38437362753171-What-is-a-Job-Success-Score); [Upwork resource](https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-to-improve-job-success-score); [Job Success insights](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/32389629156755-Job-Success-insights); [Help 211068358](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211068358)
  - Reflects overall contract history and client relationships/feedback.
  - Updated daily using 6-, 12- and 24-month windows.
  - ≥90% is "excellent" and on track for Top Rated. Below 79% makes winning work hard.
  - The "Job Success insights" page explains how to earn a first score.
- [3P] millo.co describes a different JSS cadence, which conflicts with Upwork's docs. Disregard it. — [millo](https://millo.co/upwork-job-success-score)

### Inferences
- For a 0-job freelancer, early contracts dominate the first JSS. Choose small, well-scoped fixed-price jobs with clear deliverables and responsive clients, and avoid long-running contracts that might end without feedback.
- Rising Talent rewards proposal quality and activity, so a steady stream of relevant, well-written proposals may itself help.

### Gaps
- The exact contract-count threshold before a first JSS appears was not confirmed in snippets.
- How private feedback and contracts without feedback are weighted is not stated by Upwork in the results.

## 3. Connects, boosts, ads and Freelancer Plus

### Takeaway
- Connects cost $0.15 each, sold in bundles.
- Basic accounts get 10 free Connects per month. Freelancer Plus costs $19.99/month and includes 100 Connects per month (Upwork resource page).
- Per-job proposal cost varies by job and can change while the job is posted. 2026 third-party samples put a typical proposal at about 4–16+ Connects.
- Boosted proposals are an auction for up to 4 top slots. Bids are charged only if the client interacts while you're promoted. Boost bids on competitive jobs can reach hundreds of Connects.

### Cited Findings
- Each Connect costs $0.15. Connects pay for proposals and for ads: Boosted Proposals, Boosted Profile (beta) and Availability Badge. — [Upwork Connects calculator](https://www.upwork.com/tools/connects-calculator)
- [3P] Bundles are sold at a flat rate (e.g., 10 for $1.50, 100 for $15). Purchased Connects expire 1 year after issue. Refunds when a job is cancelled without a hire. — [goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026); [Jobbers](https://www.jobbers.io/what-are-upwork-connects-and-how-much-do-they-cost/)
- **Free Connects** — [Is Upwork free](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free); [Is Freelancer Plus worth it](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-freelancer-plus-worth-it); [Help 211062888](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062888)
  - Basic is free with 10 Connects per month.
  - Freelancer Plus is $19.99/month with 100 Connects per month.
  - Plus Connects arrive on the 1st of each month, with the full charge.
  - Signing up on the 29th–31st makes the plan renew on the 1st of the following month.
- [3P] Conflicting Plus figures: 60 Connects/month, 80 Connects/month, or $14.99 with 100. Treat these as outdated or wrong next to Upwork's own page. — [Jobbers](https://www.jobbers.io/?p=143926); [aiproposer](https://aiproposer.com/learn/upwork-connect-price-2026)
- Per-job cost "varies per job and can change during the time the job is posted," based on project size, scope and market demand. The help center also describes a test that awards 12 Connects for submitting 3+ proposals, not available to all freelancers. — [Understanding and using Connects](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects)
- An Upwork community announcement once reset the base proposal cost to 2–8 Connects by job value. Undated in the snippet and likely 2023–24; probably outdated. — [Community: Boosted Proposals and Connects](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Support-Forum/Marketplace-Updates-Boosted-Proposals-and-Connects/m-p/1271430)
- [3P] 2026 estimates of proposal cost (unverified):
  - Average 12 / median 11 Connects
  - Range 4–16
  - GigRadar: 16–32 ($2.40–4.80)
  — [useoutbid](https://useoutbid.com/blog/upwork-connects-cost-per-proposal-2026); [Zenlance](https://zenlance.net/upwork-connects/); [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-connects-cost-per-hire)
- **Boosted Proposals** — [Boost your proposal](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406395531795-Boost-your-proposal); [Community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Support-Forum/Marketplace-Updates-Boosted-Proposals-and-Connects/m-p/1271430)
  - Up to 4 freelancers can boost to the top of a client's proposal list.
  - It is an auction. If you're outbid and the client didn't interact while you were promoted, you pay only the base proposal Connects.
  - The bid is charged when the client interacts with your promoted proposal.
  - Upwork claims boosting raises hire chances by up to 55% (Upwork figure, unverified).
- Forum users report boost bids of 100–700 Connects on many jobs. — [Community feedback thread](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Feedback/Marketplace-Updates-Boosted-Proposals-and-Connects/m-p/1271875)
- Availability Badge: you set a weekly Connects cap (prior notes). Boosted Profile is in beta and paid in Connects. — [Connects calculator](https://www.upwork.com/tools/connects-calculator)

### Inferences
- With 10 free Connects per month, a Basic account gets roughly 1 proposal a month at typical 2026 costs. A new freelancer will need to buy Connects or take Plus.
- Even at 100 Connects for $19.99 plus extra purchases, budget around $15–40 per month in Connects for 10–20 targeted proposals (inference from the third-party averages above).
- Boost auctions on popular Roblox jobs may be too expensive. Boost selectively on small, freshly posted, well-matched jobs.

### Gaps
- No official current figures for Boosted Profile or Availability Badge pricing; both are set by your own bid or cap.
- No official 2025–26 per-proposal Connects range found.
- Full Freelancer Plus feature list not confirmed in snippets. Older Upwork pages mention competitor bid insights and custom profile URL, but these were unverified here.

## 4. Fees and withdrawals

### Takeaway
- Since May 1, 2025 the freelancer service fee is variable, 0–15% per contract, replacing the older flat 10%.
- The fee applies to all earnings, including bonuses, on hourly, fixed-price and Catalog contracts. It is shown before the proposal or contract and locked for that contract.
- For Nigeria-based freelancers, Upwork added a $2.99 fee on Direct-to-U.S.-Bank withdrawals for non-U.S. tax addresses from Sep 1, 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Upwork's fee calculator says freelancers pay "a variable freelancer service fee ranging from 0-15% on all earnings, including bonuses," with some exceptions. The fee depends on factors such as supply and demand. — [Upwork fee calculator](https://wf-seo.upwork.com/tools/upwork-fee-calculator); [Snipework](https://snipework.com/answers/upwork-dynamic-fees-explained)
- [3P] Effective date May 1, 2025; still in place in 2026. Weighting factors are not disclosed. — [goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026); [HireInSouth](https://www.hireinsouth.com/post/how-much-does-upwork-cost)
- [3P] Typical effective rates:
  - About 10%, or 10–13%
  - 12–13% average for active freelancers in 2025 (GigRadar)
  — [goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026); [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-market-report-2026)
- [3P] Sources differ on whether the fee is shown at proposal submission or before submitting; all agree it is locked for the contract. — [goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026); [HireInSouth](https://www.hireinsouth.com/post/how-much-does-upwork-cost)
- **Withdrawals (Nigeria)** — [Cleva](https://www.getcleva.com/blog/upworks-new-2-99-withdrawal-fee-dont-let-it-cost-you-twice); [Raenest](https://www.raenest.com/blog/upwork-fees-for-african-freelancers); [TechEconomy.ng](https://techeconomy.ng/best-platform-to-withdraw-from-upwork-in-nigeria-2026)
  - $2.99 per Direct-to-U.S.-Bank withdrawal for non-U.S. tax addresses from Sep 1, 2026 (Upwork calls it cost recovery).
  - Direct to Local Bank: $0.99 per withdrawal.
  - USD wire: reported at $30–50 (sources differ).
  - Payoneer: Upwork moves funds to Payoneer, then Payoneer's FX/withdrawal fees (up to ~3%) and annual fee apply.
  - PayPal is not usable for receiving in Nigeria.
  - Caveat: Cleva and Raenest sell alternatives.

### Inferences
- Budget for an effective platform take of about 10–15% plus withdrawal/FX costs of about 1–3%.
- Withdrawing in larger, less frequent batches limits per-withdrawal fees.

### Gaps
- Upwork's own fee-schedule page wasn't fetched. Withdrawal figures rely on third-party (fintech) sources.
- The exact rules for 0% contracts (e.g., Direct Contracts / Plus) are unconfirmed.

## 5. Profile features

### Takeaway
- Specialized Profiles were removed on May 28, 2026. Freelancers now have one main profile.
- Portfolio, video intro, certifications, employment history and skills remain.
- Project Catalog still lets clients buy pre-packaged services without a proposal. Every item is manually reviewed, which reportedly takes around 10 business days.

### Cited Findings
- **Specialized Profiles removal** — [Create a Specialized Profile (help)](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013750068-Create-a-Specialized-Profile)
  - Not available as of May 28, 2026, because "clients now prefer to understand your full range of skills and experience in one place."
  - Portfolio items, earnings and work history moved to the main profile. Titles, overviews and skills did not transfer and are not retrievable.
  - Proposals sent from specialized profiles stayed visible to clients until June 28, 2026.
  - Upwork says search visibility and rankings are unaffected.
- **Required profile fields** — [Sample Profiles and Best Practices](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063208-Sample-Profiles-and-Best-Practices); [Help 360016144974](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016144974)
  - Photo, overview, at least one employment history entry and at least one skill.
  - A 100% complete profile is needed for badges.
  - Video intro is added from the profile sidebar and helps "build rapport."
  - Portfolio: be selective, show skills and tools used, no contact details, recommended image size 1000×750 px.
  - Listing certifications adds credibility.
- [3P] Upwork Skills Certifications are described as verifications of skills; claimed ranking boosts are unverified. — [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-skills-certifications)
- Project Catalog: clients buy pre-packaged services without a proposal. A 2024 community reply said reviews took at least 10 business days (older; may have changed). — [Community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/New-to-Upwork/Get-Profile-Feedback-from-Experienced-Freelancers-on-Upwork/m-p/1534262/highlight/true)
- Title/review rules are already in prior notes: "You will get…" ≤75 chars, rejection for typos, poor images or contact info. — [Help 360058122033](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058122033)

### Inferences
- With one profile, the title and overview must cover the Roblox niche directly; there is no separate "specialized" profile anymore.
- Category/specialty selection on the main profile and Catalog items are now the main way to signal niche.
- Earlier "skills tests" were retired years ago. Upwork's current "Skills Certifications" (where available for a skill) are the replacement; their availability for Roblox/Luau is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- No current Upwork documentation found for seller-side Consultations (paid calls) in 2026.
- Whether Skills Certifications exist for Roblox/Luau or game dev is unknown.
- Current category/specialty limits (how many) not confirmed.

## 6. Account risks (ToS, verification, suspension triggers)

### Takeaway
The highest-risk behaviors for a new account are:
- Sharing contact details or moving payment off-platform. A 24-month non-circumvention period applies, unless the conversion fee is paid.
- Identity-verification mismatches between ID, profile name, tax form and payout method.
- Duplicate or shared accounts.
- Feedback building (covered in prior notes).

Mass identity-related suspensions have happened (Ukraine, July 2025).

### Cited Findings
- **Identity verification** — [Upwork Legal Center](https://www.upwork.com/legal)
  - The verification badge is valid for 3 years; re-verification may be required.
  - Connects or service fees used for mandatory identity verification are non-refundable regardless of outcome.
- [3P] The non-circumvention window is 24 months for relationships started on Upwork. Discussing off-platform contact or payment in Messages is itself a violation. The opt-out conversion fee is about 13.5% of estimated annual earnings (vendor estimate). — [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-account-suspended); [Freelance Filter ToS guide 2026](https://www.freelancefilter.com/upwork-freelancer-terms-of-service-2026)
- [3P] Detection and account rules:
  - Upwork uses device fingerprinting and IP checks to find duplicate accounts.
  - Names must match across ID, withdrawal method and tax info.
  - Do not create a second account while suspended.
  - Do not contact clients off-platform about a suspension.
  — [GoLogin](https://gologin.com/blog/upwork-account-suspended/); [UpHunt appeal guide](https://uphunt.io/blog/upwork-account-suspended-recovery-appeal-guide-2026); [goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-account-suspended-what-to-do-next)
- July 2025: Ukrainian freelancers were mass-suspended after identity checks. Upwork began unblocking after a government appeal. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upwork)
- Portfolio items must not contain contact details, and Catalog projects with pre-contract contact info are rejected. — [Help 211063208](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063208-Sample-Profiles-and-Best-Practices)

### Inferences
- For a Nigerian freelancer, the main avoidable risks are:
  - Using a VPN or a location that doesn't match the ID.
  - Letting family share the device or account.
  - A name mismatch between the ID and the payout method (bank/Payoneer).
  - Accepting a client's request to "talk on Discord/Telegram" before a contract.
- Roblox clients commonly use Discord. Keep all pre-contract communication on Upwork. After the contract, use Upwork messages for anything payment-related.

### Gaps
- Upwork's official current Terms of Service text was not fetched. Conversion-fee amount and non-circumvention wording come from third-party sources.
- No official data on suspension rates or Nigeria-specific verification issues in 2026.

# Upwork Marketplace State 2025–2026: Metrics, Demand Trends, AI Effects, Game Dev/Roblox

> Method note: Every WebFetch call failed (DNS errors for sec.gov, investors.upwork.com, staffingindustry.com), so **all findings come from search-engine snippets and summaries**, not full-page reads. Figures attributed to primary sources (Upwork/Fiverr releases, SEC filings) were seen only in snippets. Spot-check them before publishing. Research date: 2026-10-24. Q3 2026 results were not found and are probably not out yet; Upwork usually reports in early November.

## 1. Upwork company metrics 2025–2026 and strategy

### Takeaway
Upwork is shrinking in client count but earning more per client and more per transaction. Active clients are down about 4% a year, while take rate and GSV per client are rising. In May 2026 it cut about 24% of its workforce and lowered 2026 revenue guidance, citing faster AI automation, a weak labor market and fewer Google referrals. Its growth bets are AI-related work, the Uma AI agent, Business Plus for SMBs, and Lifted for enterprise.

### Cited Findings
**Full year and Q4 2025**
- Full-year 2025 revenue was a record $787.8M. — [Upwork Q4/FY2025 release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial); [Barchart copy](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/114482/upwork-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial-results)
- GSV from AI-related work passed $300M on an annualized basis in Q4 2025, up more than 50% year over year. — [Upwork Q4/FY2025 release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial)
- Business Plus (the SMB offering): Q4 2025 GSV rose 24% quarter over quarter, and 38% of its active clients were new to Upwork. A secondary summary says Business Plus was about 2.5% of GSV at end-2025 and is expected to at least double that share in 2026. — [Upwork Q4 release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial); [Fintool summary](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/UPWK/earnings/Q4%202025)
- Q3 2025 marketplace take rate was about 18.9%. In Q2 2025 it rose about 50 bps year over year, which the company attributed to value-based pricing and supply/demand experiments. — [Fintool Q3 2025](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/UPWK/earnings/Q3%202025); [Fintool Q2 2025](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/UPWK/earnings/Q2%202025)
- Lifted launched in August 2025 as a wholly owned enterprise subsidiary. — [Fintool](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/UPWK)
- Initial 2026 guidance, per a secondary summary: GSV growth of 4–6%, revenue growth of 6–8%, and an adjusted EBITDA margin of about 29%. — [Fintool](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/UPWK)
- Uma: Upwork rebranded it as an "AI Work Agent" that scopes work and matches talent. A secondary outlet credits Uma with about $100M of incremental 2025 GSV/revenue; this is unverified. — [Upwork release: Uma becomes AI Work Agent](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-evolves-uma-ai-ai-work-agent-advances-human-ai); [Munich Startup](https://insights.munich-startup.de/news/feed/upwork-s-ai-agent-uma-drives-100m-in-incremental-revenue-as-company-pivots-to-enterprise)

**Q1 2026 (reported May 2026)**
- Revenue was about $195.5M, up 1.4% year over year. GSV was about $987M, roughly flat. Active clients were about 784K, down from 832K, which comes from GigRadar's reading of the release. GSV per active client rose 5%. The CEO described a "challenging demand environment." — [Seeking Alpha PR](https://seekingalpha.com/pr/20507035); [GigRadar market report 2026](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-market-report-2026)
- Lifted finished its technology and operations integration and planned to start migrating its first wave of enterprise customers to the new platform by end of June 2026. — [Kavout](https://www.kavout.com/market-lens/what-s-driving-upwork-s-recent-volatility-and-shifting-outlook) (via search snippet)
- Restructuring was announced May 7, 2026: about a 24% cut to the total workforce, $16–23M in pre-tax charges, about $70M in annualized opex savings, and about $40M realized in 2026. — [Upwork 10-Q Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001627475/000162747526000047/upwk-20260630.htm); [Yahoo call summary](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/upwork-inc-q2-2026-earnings-123000363.html)

**Q2 2026 (reported Aug 10, 2026)**
- GSV was $966.4M, down 4%. Active clients were 763K, down 4%. GSV per active client was $5,230, up 5%, the eighth straight quarter of sequential growth. Revenue was $191.7M, down 2%. Adjusted EBITDA was $64.1M, up 12%, a 33.4% margin. — [Upwork 8-K Q2 2026 press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001627475/000162747526000046/upwork2q26-pressrelease.htm); [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/upwork-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-08-10/)
- Take rate was 19.8%. Management expects it to keep rising in the second half, driven by dynamic pricing on freelancer fees. Analysts also linked the increase to more competition for jobs. — [Yahoo Q2 call summary](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/upwork-inc-q2-2026-earnings-123000363.html); [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/17/upwork-upwk-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- AI-related GSV rose more than 22% to about $330M (likely annualized), though that growth is slowing. — [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001627475/000162747526000046/upwork2q26-pressrelease.htm); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/upwork-q2-2026-ai-disruption-guidance-cut)
- Full-year 2026 revenue guidance was cut to $730–750M from $760–790M. Adjusted EBITDA guidance is $225–235M. Management assumes "a heightened pace of AI-related automation and no improvements in the labor market," plus weaker Google/SEO referrals. Shares fell about 20% after hours. — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/upwork-q2-2026-ai-disruption-guidance-cut); [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/earnings/2026/upwork-navigates-cross-currents-as-ai-reshapes-freelance-demand)
- New $150M revolving credit facility, intended to repay the August 2026 convertible notes. — [Yahoo summary](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/upwork-inc-q2-2026-earnings-123000363.html)
- Conflicting headline: Staffing Industry Analysts titled its article "Upwork Q2 revenue falls 17%". That contradicts the company-reported −2%; it may refer to a different line item or be an error. — [SIA](https://www.staffingindustry.com/news/global-daily-news/upwork-q2-revenue-falls-17-though-ai-related-work-provides-bright-spot)
- Unverified community report from August 2026 that Upwork reduced client advertising. — [Snipework](https://snipework.com/news/upwork-advertising-client-decline)

### Inferences
- Fewer clients spending more each, a rising take rate, and "increased competition for jobs" together point to a tougher market for a typical freelancer: fewer buyers, more bidders, and higher fees, which come from dynamic pricing.
- Upwork is moving upmarket (Business Plus, Lifted enterprise, larger AI projects). Freelancers who can serve SMB and enterprise clients on larger, AI-adjacent projects fit where the platform is heading. Low-budget one-off gigs fit it least.
- Revenue guidance of $730–750M implies about a 5–7% drop from 2025's $787.8M. This is my own calculation.

### Gaps
- No total active-freelancer count for 2025–2026 was found. Upwork no longer prominently discloses it. Investor materials cited about 250K "AI experts."
- I could not confirm full-year 2025 GSV or full-year take rate.
- Q3 2026 results were not found and have likely not been released.
- I could not identify the specific features or packaging of Business Plus beyond the GSV figures.

## 2. Demand trends by category (AI, game dev, Roblox)

### Takeaway
On Upwork, AI-applied skills are growing fastest: AI video, AI integration, data annotation and chatbots. Core skills such as full-stack, VA, data analytics and design are holding steady. I found no Upwork data on game development or Roblox. Outside Upwork, the Roblox creator economy and studio spending on freelancers are growing.

### Cited Findings
- In-Demand Skills 2026 (published Feb 4, 2026): the top AI-applied skills grew 109% year over year. AI video generation/editing grew 329%, AI integration 178%, AI data annotation/labeling 154%, and AI chatbot development 71%. — [Upwork press release](https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upworks-in-demand-skills-2026-demand-for-top-ai-skills-more-than-doubles-as-ai-is-embedded-into-everyday-work); [Upwork IR](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-demand-skills-2026-demand-top-ai-skills-more-doubles-ai)
- In coding/web dev, the fastest growers were AI Integration, AI Chatbot Development and Firmware Development (+14%). In admin/customer service they were Ecommerce Management (+130%), Medical VA (+44%) and Supply Chain PM (+37%). Full-stack dev, general VA, data analytics and graphic design were stable. — [Upwork press release](https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upworks-in-demand-skills-2026-demand-for-top-ai-skills-more-than-doubles-as-ai-is-embedded-into-everyday-work)
- AI-related GSV grew 60% in 2024, more than 50% in Q4 2025, and 22% in Q2 2026. — [Upwork Future Workforce Index release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-study-finds-1-4-us-skilled-knowledge-workers-now-work); [Q4 2025 release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/upwork-q2-2026-ai-disruption-guidance-cut)
- Roblox: DevEx paid out about $1B in 2025 per Roblox's Q3 2025 report, versus $923M in 2024. Statista puts 2025 at about $1.5B, which conflicts. Roblox raised the DevEx rate by 8.5%. Top-1,000 creators averaged $1.1M, up 40%. Creator Rewards replaced Engagement-Based Payouts in July 2025, per the 10-K. — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Roblox-continues-to-reward-game-content-creators-as-payouts-cross-1-billion.1157555.0.html); [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1376672/roblox-developer-payout); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/robloxs-top-developers-average-339-million-in-annual-earnings); [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- Mellow (a contractor-payments vendor) reports that studio spending on game-dev freelancers grew 55% globally and contractor numbers grew 21% worldwide. One- to three-month contracts are growing fastest. 54% of freelancers reported difficulty finding suitable work. — [Mellow research](https://mellow.io/news/mellow-research-freelancing-in-game-development); [Mellow 2025 report](https://mellow.io/gamedev-research-25)
- There are signs of Roblox's own corporate layoffs in 2026, from a recruiter blog. — [Kore1](https://www.kore1.com/roblox-layoffs-2026/)
- Sample Upwork Roblox listings include a $200 fixed-price Luau scripting job and a $1,600 fixed-price expert Roblox developer job, which shows small budgets are common. — [Outlook Respawn guide](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-guides/freelance-game-development-how-to-find-clients-and-set-rates); [Upwork job example](https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022065966427339980545)

### Inferences
- Game dev did not appear among Upwork's headline growth skills, which suggests it is a niche, stable category rather than a growth driver on Upwork.
- Roblox spending is growing, but much Roblox hiring happens off Upwork, inside the Roblox ecosystem (see section 5).
- A Roblox developer who adds AI-adjacent skills such as AI NPCs, chatbot integration, or AI-assisted asset pipelines would line up with Upwork's fastest-growing demand.

### Gaps
- I found no Upwork or GigRadar data on the volume or trend of game-dev, Roblox or Luau job postings.
- Upwork's In-Demand Skills report did not show category-level figures for game development in the snippets I saw.

## 3. How AI is changing freelancing on Upwork

### Takeaway
AI is removing low-complexity work (writing, translation, simple images) and lowering what those contracts pay, while AI-skilled freelancers earn premiums. Proposals are flooded with AI-written text, so clients increasingly filter for personalized, demonstrably human applications.

### Cited Findings
- Upwork says its outlook assumes "a heightened pace of AI-related automation." — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/upwork-q2-2026-ai-disruption-guidance-cut)
- Upwork Research Institute: freelancers doing AI work earn 34% more per hour. Lower-complexity generative AI and creative work saw per-contract earnings fall 13% even as contract starts rose. The publication date is unconfirmed. — [Axios (June 2025)](https://www.axios.com/2025/06/30/ai-job-vibe-coding-upwork); [Upwork IR PDF](https://investors.upwork.com/node/13166/pdf)
- Axios (June 2025) reported that AI job substitution on Upwork is "real, but narrow." — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/06/30/ai-job-vibe-coding-upwork)
- An academic study using Upwork data found that after ChatGPT's release, writing freelancers' monthly jobs fell 2% and earnings fell 5.2%, with image work hit harder. Translation hourly rates fell more than 20% between November 2023 and February 2024 (secondary source). — [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/earnings/2026/upwork-navigates-cross-currents-as-ai-reshapes-freelance-demand); [Skillscouter](https://skillscouter.com/freelance-statistics/)
- Upwork's Human+Agent Productivity Index found human-AI collaboration raises project completion by up to 70%. — [Upwork press release](https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upworks-in-demand-skills-2026-demand-for-top-ai-skills-more-than-doubles-as-ai-is-embedded-into-everyday-work)
- 54% of freelancers report advanced AI proficiency, compared with 38% of full-time employees. — [Upwork Future Workforce Index](https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upwork-study-finds-1-in-4-u-s-skilled-knowledge-workers-now-work-independently-generating-1-5-trillion-in-earnings)
- AI-written proposals: vendor GigRadar claims 80–90% of proposals contain near-identical AI language. Stock phrases ("Thanks for posting", "I have experience with") correlate with lower reply rates. Clients plant keyword traps in job posts. All of these come from vendor or blog sources with commercial interests. — [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/ai-proposals-upwork); [Tyler Willis](https://tylerewillis.com/blog/ai-proposals-upwork-spam-filter-reply-rate)
- Upwork policy, per third-party summaries: AI assistance is allowed, but proposals must accurately reflect the freelancer's capabilities, and mass automated submission without oversight is prohibited. — [GetMany](https://getmany.com/blog/upwork-ai-policy); [GigUpHQ](https://giguphq.com/blog/upwork-ai-proposals-banned-2026)

### Inferences
- Freelancers should expect less simple, standalone work and more work that integrates AI or fixes AI output. Clients expect faster delivery at the same or lower price.
- Generic AI-written proposals are likely to be filtered out by clients, and possibly by Upwork. Specific, verifiable proof of work, such as Roblox game links, videos and visit counts, sets a proposal apart.

### Gaps
- Upwork has not published how, or whether, it algorithmically detects AI-written proposals.
- I could not read Upwork's own Terms of Service text on AI use directly.

## 4. Competition, rates, and global freelancer distribution (Nigeria/Africa)

### Takeaway
Competition on Upwork is rising: fewer clients, plus management's own reference to "increased competition for jobs." Game-dev rates on Upwork cluster low, around $15–35/hr in one guide. African freelancing is growing fast, with Nigeria up about 130% over five years across platforms, but I found no Upwork-specific country share.

### Cited Findings
- Analysts tied take-rate expansion to "dynamic pricing on freelancer fees and increased competition for jobs within the marketplace." — [Yahoo Q2 2026 summary](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/upwork-inc-q2-2026-earnings-123000363.html)
- Game-dev Upwork median is about $28/hr, with most postings $15–35/hr. Platform freelancers earn 20–30% less than those with direct client pipelines (secondary guide). — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-guides/freelance-game-development-how-to-find-clients-and-set-rates)
- Vendor rate guides put Unity developers at $25–275/hr, and $100–250/hr for multiplayer/VR specialists. Unreal, multiplayer and technical art are cited as the highest-paying specialties. — [goLance](https://golance.com/hiring/best-freelance-unity-3d-developers-hourly-rate); [Jobbers](https://www.jobbers.io/?p=75648)
- Platform freelancing growth over five years: Nigeria about 130%, Kenya more than 200%, South Africa under 10%. — [Ecofin Agency (Jan 2026)](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/1501-51945-africa-s-digital-freelancers-growth-opportunity-and-precarity)
- Africa's freelance tech market is projected to grow from $7.32B in 2024 to $37.71B by 2034. This is a projection. — [CB Insights / Terawork](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/terawork/)
- Filipino freelancers on Upwork report competing with AI. — [People Matters SEA](https://sea.peoplemattersglobal.com/article/life-at-work/filipino-freelancers-on-upwork-find-themselves-competing-with-ai-45311)
- In the US, 28% of knowledge workers freelance, earning $1.5T in 2024. Freelancers' median income is $85K, versus $80K for full-time employees. — [Upwork Future Workforce Index](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-study-finds-1-4-us-skilled-knowledge-workers-now-work)

### Inferences
- A Nigerian Roblox developer competes in a crowded, price-sensitive pool. Differentiation (portfolio, niche, speed, AI-assisted workflows) matters more than price.
- Fees rising through dynamic pricing combined with low game-dev budgets could squeeze net rates further.

### Gaps
- I found no data on freelancers per category on Upwork, or on Nigeria's or Africa's share of Upwork freelancers.
- I found no verified Roblox- or Luau-specific rate data.

## 5. Alternatives and complements for Roblox developers

### Takeaway
Upwork charges freelancers 0–15% per contract (about 10% typical) plus paid Connects, and is moving upmarket. Fiverr is shrinking in buyers but rising in spend per buyer, with a roughly 28% take rate. Contra charges no commission but is not game-focused. Most Roblox-native hiring happens on Roblox Talent Hub, the DevForum and Discord servers. Those channels are close to the work but have weaker payment protection.

### Cited Findings
- Upwork fees: since May 1, 2025 the freelancer fee has been a variable 0–15% per contract (it replaced the old 20%/10%/5% tiers). The rate is shown at proposal time and fixed for the contract; about 10% is typical, per third-party blogs. Connects cost $0.15 each, and a proposal typically costs 2–16. — [goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026); [Wittypen](https://wittypen.com/blog/new-upwork-connects-pricing/); [Jobbers](https://www.jobbers.io/upwork-fees-explained-hidden-costs-freelancers-dont-know-about/)
- Fiverr Q2 2026: 2.7M annual active buyers, down 21.9% from 3.4M. Spend per buyer was $368, up 15.6%. Revenue was $97.8M, down 10%. Fiverr is shifting upmarket, and clients completing $1,000+ projects grew 13%. Its marketplace take rate is about 27.7–28%. — [Fiverr Q2 2026 release](https://www.fiverr.com/news/fiverr-q2-2026-earnings); [SelfEmployed on Q1](https://www.selfemployed.com/news/fiverr-q1-2026-marketplace-pivot/)
- Contra does not charge freelancers commission. It makes money from Pro subscriptions ($29/mo or $199/yr) and client-side fees. Contra Payments launched in February 2026. Its focus is designers, Webflow, video and animation. — [Contra](https://contra.com/commission-free); [TechCrunch 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/11/02/independent-worker-network-contra-goes-commission-free-as-it-secures-new-nea-led-capital)
- Roblox-native channels: Talent Hub (official, with verified creator experience), DevForum, and Discord servers including HiddenDevs, Hire Developers, RoDevs and Group Recruiting Plaza, plus newer marketplaces such as PeakDevs. — [DevForum: commissions](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-start-making-commissions/1249757); [DevForum: PeakDevs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/peakdevs-%E2%80%94-find-roblox-developers-and-opportunities/4745149)
- One low-quality review also covers Upwork alternatives. — [goLance review](https://golance.com/blogs/is-upwork-legit-2026-review) (goLance is a competitor)

### Inferences
- For Roblox work, Upwork's strength is payment protection and access to non-Roblox-native clients, such as brands and agencies building Roblox experiences. Roblox-native studios mostly hire through Talent Hub and Discord, often paying in Robux or percentage revenue share.
- Upwork's fee (about 10%) is lower than Fiverr's effective take of about 20% from sellers plus buyer fees. Upwork adds Connects costs, though.
- A sensible strategy is Upwork for higher-budget brand and agency clients alongside Talent Hub, DevForum and Discord for ecosystem work.

### Gaps
- I could not verify Fiverr's seller fee (widely reported as 20%) or Fiverr Go details from a primary source.
- I found no fee or scam-rate data for Talent Hub or Discord commissions, and no client-quality comparison data across platforms.

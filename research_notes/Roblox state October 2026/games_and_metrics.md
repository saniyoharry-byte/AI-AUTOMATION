## 2. How fast viral hits rise and decay; evergreen games

### Takeaway
Viral Roblox hits reach peak within weeks and lose more than 90–99% of peak CCU within about 6–12 months. Steal An Egg went from launch to about 14M CCU in roughly 8 weeks. Steal a Brainrot dropped from 25.8M to about 0.1–0.2M CCU within a year. A small set of evergreen games keeps holding hundreds of thousands of CCU for years: Brookhaven, Adopt Me!, Blox Fruits and MM2.

### Cited Findings
- **Steal a Brainrot**: peaked at about 25.8M CCU on Oct 11, 2025. One early-Oct 2026 report puts it at about 90K–205K, a drop of more than 99%, while total visits keep climbing to about 73.9B. A May 2026 article still had it at "hundreds of thousands" daily. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot), [Lootbar](https://www.lootbar.com/blog/en/steal-a-brainrot-roblox-top-charts.html), [GreyJournal](https://greyjournal.net/hustle/how-much-does-steal-a-brainrot-make/)
- Steal a Brainrot revenue is estimated at $1.4M–$4.4M per month, "not $11M". This is a third-party estimate with an unclear period. — [GreyJournal](https://greyjournal.net/hustle/how-much-does-steal-a-brainrot-make/)
- **Grow a Garden**: peaked at 22.3M CCU on Aug 23, 2025, and has "been struggling to clear half that" since. It was still about 1–1.2M daily CCU in May 2026. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden), [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/mmo/with-21-million-concurrent-players-roblox-hit-grow-a-garden-is-bigger-than-half-of-steam-combined-but-players-are-already-flooding-another-far-weirder-competitor/), [StudioKrew May 2026](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-may-2026/)
- Grow a Garden 2's player-count drop was partly caused by Roblox removing large numbers of bot accounts. Some reported declines therefore reflect bots, not lost real players. — [MMOexp](https://www.mmoexp.com/News/grow-a-garden-2-is-losing-players-what-really-happened-to-the-roblox-hit.html)
- **99 Nights in the Forest**: 14.15M peak in Oct 2025, down to about 0.13–0.39M in Oct 2026 (about −97 to −99%). It is still a top-6 to top-10 game. — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/79546208627805), [RoCodes](https://rocodes.gg/codes/99-nights-in-the-forest)
- **Steal An Egg**: about 1.3M CCU on Sept 9 and about 14M at the Sept 19–26 peak, a jump driven by a crossover event. It fell to about 1–1.6M by mid-to-late October (about −90% within 4 weeks of peak), but it remains #1. — [RoVitals via search](https://rowatcher.com/games/10563114921/steal-an-egg), [LiveCounts](https://livecounts.nl/roblox-game-player-count/?id=107778070777162), [clip.dor.gg](https://clip.dor.gg/en/blog/steal-an-egg-roblox-guide)
- RoWatcher analysis: about **80% of games that peaked in the top 50 during 2024 were not in the top 200 by early 2026**. It sorts games into three paths: sustained, graceful decline, and cliff-drop. The first **90 days** after a viral peak are treated as the window that decides survival. This is single-outlet analysis. — [RoWatcher "One Year on the Charts"](https://rowatcher.com/news/one-year-on-the-charts-what-really-happens-after-a-roblox-game-goes-viral)
- Evergreen games such as Brookhaven RP, Adopt Me! and Blox Fruits remain strong. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/one-year-on-the-charts-what-really-happens-after-a-roblox-game-goes-viral), [Medium: "Why are these old Roblox games still alive?"](https://medium.com/@evanhertzfeldt/why-are-these-roblox-games-still-alive-92a566dc94f8)

### Inferences
- Rule of thumb from these cases:
  - Viral hits reach peak in 4–10 weeks.
  - They fall about 80–90% within 1–3 months of peak.
  - A year later they settle at about 1% of peak (0.1–0.4M CCU) if they survive.
- Spikes of 5–10× are often driven by events: crossovers, admin abuse events and limited-time updates. Peak CCU overstates a game's sustainable base.
- One viral game per year can now carry a large share of the platform's quarterly engagement. This makes Roblox's results "hit-dependent" (see section 3).

### Gaps
- No Naavik, Deconstructor of Fun or GameRefinery 2026 cohort data on trend lifespans was found. RoWatcher is the only systematic source, and its methods are unverified.
- No consistent long-run CCU time series could be retrieved, since Rolimons and RoMonitor were not fetchable.

## 3. Platform metrics: Q1–Q2 2026 actuals, Q3 2026 guidance, and the DAU decline

### Takeaway
DAU peaked at about 152M in Q3 2025, then fell to 132M in Q1 2026 and **123M in Q2 2026**, though Q2 was still +10% YoY. Hours engaged fell from 40B to 29B. Management blames:
- the global chat age-check rollout (Jan 2026)
- discovery-algorithm changes that favor retention over viral "slop"
- engagement shifting away from the high-monetizing 2025 viral hits
- the Russia ban (Dec 2025)

Q3 2026 bookings are guided **down 14–18% YoY**. Results land Oct 29, and the market expects Steal An Egg to lift engagement.

### Cited Findings

Reasons given: safety-measure headwinds, age-check friction at sign-up, reduced viral sharing, weaker app-store sentiment and slower new-user acquisition. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/roblox-cuts-2026-guidance-age-123523142.html), [Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/roblox-q1-2026-earnings/)

## 4. Regional mix of users

### Takeaway
APAC is Roblox's largest and fastest-growing region by users. Growth in 2026 comes almost entirely from APAC and the Rest of World, while Europe is flat. Monetization is still concentrated in the US and Canada.

### Cited Findings
- Q2 2026 DAU by region (secondary sources; the official supplemental PDF returned only chart headings) — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q2-2026-slides-bookings-growth-slows-to-8-amid-user-decline-93CH-4826401), [Q2 Supplemental Materials](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/Roblox-Q2-2026-Supplemental-Materials.pdf):
  - APAC: 41M (+15% YoY)
  - Rest of World: 34M (+15%)
  - Europe: 26M (+1%)
  - US & Canada: 22M (+6%)
- Q2 2026 hours engaged by region (one report) — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q2-2026-slides-bookings-growth-slows-to-8-amid-user-decline-93CH-4826401):
  - APAC about 10B (+9%)
  - Rest of World about 8B (+11%)
  - Europe about 6B (**−5%**, the only declining region)
  - US & Canada about 6B (+1%)
- Conflicting figures: DemandSage gives APAC 11.2B hours, US & Canada 6.3B hours, and different DAU numbers (Europe 34M, US & Canada 24M). It is likely stale or mislabeled, so treat it as less reliable. — [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/how-many-people-play-roblox/)
- US & Canada brought in $846M of $1,469M in Q2 2026 (the source does not say whether this is revenue or bookings; it is likely revenue). The US alone was about 54%. Per-hour monetization is falling among young North Americans. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-revenue-rises-36-yy-but-young-north-americans-see-falling-per-hour-monetisation/), [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q2-2026-slides-bookings-growth-slows-to-8-amid-user-decline-93CH-4826401)

### Inferences
- About 61% of DAU (APAC + RoW) brings in well under half of revenue. User growth in lower-ARPU regions plus weaker per-hour spend in the US explains much of the gap between user growth (+10%) and bookings growth (+8%).
- Europe's flat DAU and falling hours are consistent with age-check and regulatory friction there. This is not confirmed by management.

### Gaps
- No country-level split was found (e.g., Indonesia, Philippines, Brazil, India), and Africa is not broken out (it falls inside "Rest of World").
- Regional DAU for Q3 2026 is not yet available.

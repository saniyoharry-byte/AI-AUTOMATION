## 1. Channels: where Roblox developers get hired and how the channels differ

### Takeaway
Hiring is split across channels. The **DevForum and Discord servers** (e.g., Hidden Developers) are where most of the Robux-paid, commission and rev-share work happens. **Talent Hub** is Roblox's official board, but developers say job quality is mixed and it has no built-in payment. **Upwork/Fiverr** serve outside-the-community clients (YouTubers, brands, first-time founders) paying in USD, and are heavy on low-budget and non-game work. **Studio careers pages** (Gamefam, Voldex, etc.) are where salaried USD jobs are.

### Cited Findings
- A vendor blog (Game-Ace, updated July 2026) calls Talent Hub the most direct route for hiring a single Roblox role. It recommends agencies or outsourced studios when a project needs several roles at once. — [Game-Ace](https://game-ace.com/blog/how-to-hire-the-best-roblox-game-developer/)
- Talent Hub has had **no built-in way to pay people**. A 2023-era thread said there "actually is no way to pay users via the Talent Hub", and the workarounds were group payouts and gamepasses. A request for in-Hub Robux payment was answered with "no active plans" (thread over a year old, current status unverified). — [DevForum: Payment in talent hub](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/payment-in-talent-hub/2392274); [DevForum: Better Payment Method for Robux](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/better-payment-method-for-robux-hopefully-on-the-talent-hub/2394229)
- Talent Hub job posts can set compensation as **USD, Robux or Percentage** (shown by a bug report where the currency switched from Robux back to USD on save). — [DevForum bug report](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/unable-to-change-compensation-to-robux-from-usd-on-talent-hub/4544707)
- Developers complain about Talent Hub job quality. A 2024 thread says listings marked USD actually offered Robux or percentage with no upfront money. A 2025 thread describes rev-share offers with no budget. Other threads: "It's way too hard finding quality projects on the Talent Hub". — [DevForum: My stance on the Talent Hub](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-stance-on-the-talent-hub/2889391); [DevForum: Talent Hub – Thoughts?](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/talent-hub-thoughts/3988817); [DevForum: too hard finding quality projects](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/its-way-too-hard-finding-quality-projects-on-the-talent-hub/1747965?page=2) (snippets only)
- **Hidden Developers** is described as one of the largest Roblox dev Discord servers (tens of thousands of members). A DevForum "how to find good developers" post ranks it just below the DevForum as a hiring venue. — [DevForum: How To Find Good Developers](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-find-good-developers/712311); [creation.dev Discord list](https://www.creation.dev/tools/discord-servers)
- Other Discord or third-party marketplaces advertise hiring/commissions: VisibleDevs (Discord), Developer's Spawn (offers "free background checks" via a middleman), and Peak Devs (new marketplace announced on DevForum, 2026). — [Discord: VisibleDevs](https://discord.com/servers/visibledevs-or-roblox-developers-or-roblox-studio-1222601584945528842); [DevForum: Developer Spawn](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-developer-spawn/707385); [DevForum: Peak Devs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/peak-devs-new-roblox-developer-marketplace/4741372)
- A sales/middleman layer is emerging. A 2026 DevForum post from Toybox Entertainment recruits "commission scouts" paid **3–15% of each closed deal**, paid in USD and Robux. — [DevForum: Hiring Commission Scouts](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/hiring-commission-scouts-payment-usd-robux/4748485); [DevForum: Toybox Entertainment](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/%F0%9F%9F%A2open-toybox-entertainment-recruiting-developers-of-all-skill-levels/4760536)
- Studio careers postings found:
  - **Gamefam:** "Programmer 3 – Luau", remote USA, senior.
  - **Voldex:** "Lead Software Engineer – Brookhaven", remote-first. Requires 3–5+ years of Roblox experience and 1+ year leading a team of 3 or more. Benefits listed: retirement, health, profit sharing, stock options.
  - [Built In: Gamefam Programmer 3 – Luau](https://builtin.com/job/programmer-3-luau/3430390); [startup.jobs: Voldex Lead SWE Brookhaven](https://startup.jobs/lead-software-engineer-brookhaven-voldex-games-8759654); [Built In Boston: Voldex](https://www.builtinboston.com/job/lead-software-engineer-brookhaven/10219807)
- Upwork (from prior notes): out of 40 recent Roblox jobs, only ~38% were game dev/scripting. 22% were animation for YouTube/TikTok shorts and 15% were UGC/3D, and there were no pure UI jobs. — local file `research/roblox-jobs-oct2026.md`
- The prior notes also cite a 2026 DevForum post where a studio opens applications for "Software Engineer / Game Developer" roles. — [DevForum 2026 applications](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/2026-software-engineer-game-developer-applications-now-open/4632709)

### Inferences
- The two markets barely overlap. In-community clients (DevForum/Discord/Talent Hub) think in Robux and percentages and expect a DevForum portfolio. Off-platform clients (Upwork/Fiverr) think in USD, often aren't game developers, and buy content (shorts animation, UGC, thumbnails) as much as game code.
- Twitter/X is widely used by Roblox devs for portfolios and studio announcements, but **no source in this search confirmed** that it is a hiring channel. Treat that as anecdotal.
- Middleman and scout roles, plus "agency pool" posts on Upwork (prior notes, #38 at $8–15/hr), suggest agencies are packaging cheap Roblox labor for outside clients.

### Gaps
- No usage stats for Talent Hub (number of jobs or users) and no official 2026 Talent Hub docs: create.roblox.com and talent.roblox.com were unreachable.
- Hidden Developers' own rules (payment-proof requirements, Robux/USD minimums, scam reporting) were not found.
- No careers-page data for Super League, Melon or Dubit. Searches returned only general Roblox salary aggregators.
- No Reddit r/robloxgamedev posts surfaced in search.

## 2. Demand by role in 2026

### Takeaway
Scripters/programmers remain the core paid role. The work increasingly involves live-service skills (LiveOps, economy, retention), not just "Lua scripting". Builders are being absorbed into 3D modeling and world building. Off-platform, demand for **animation for short-form video and UGC/avatar assets** is notably large. I found **no direct evidence that demand for UI or monetization specialists is growing**. UI mostly appears bundled inside dev jobs.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 hiring guides describe the work as mainly live-service, "part engineering, part LiveOps and part economy design, whatever the title says". LiveOps specialists run the calendar of events, limited items and balance changes. — [Juego Studio 2026 guide](https://www.juegostudio.com/blog/hire-roblox-game-developer); [Innovecs 2026 guide](https://www.innovecsgames.com/blog/roblox-game-developers-for-hire/) (vendor blogs; snippets only)
- Strong candidates are expected to bring system-level thinking, multiplayer architecture, cross-device performance optimization and "LiveOps readiness", not just Lua. — [Innovecs](https://www.innovecsgames.com/blog/roblox-game-developers-for-hire/)
- A DevForum commenter says builder pay is shrinking: "builder" is becoming "world builder", and much of the role is becoming 3D modeling. — [DevForum: Looking for more devs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/looking-for-more-devs/3907125) (snippet)
- Animators are usually hired per commission, as needed, and rarely on percentage. — [DevForum: Am I offering a good price for animators](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/am-i-offering-a-good-price-for-animators-per-animation/4071933)
- Typical team composition from a 2025-era rev-share recruitment (Veil): programmers 10%, builders/modelers/animators 6% each, UI designer 4%, SFX 3%. The weighting shows programmers valued highest and UI near the bottom. — [DevForum: Looking for more devs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/looking-for-more-devs/3907125)
- Upwork Oct 2026 sample (prior notes): no standalone UI jobs. UI showed up only inside dev jobs ("mobile-friendly UI", "Gamepass Shop"). — local `research/roblox-jobs-oct2026.md`
- Macro context: Q2 2026 bookings grew only +8% against +36% revenue growth, which Roblox attributed to lower monetization per hour. Q3 bookings are guided −14% to −18%. — [Roblox Q2 2026 earnings (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm), as summarized by [sqmagazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-statistics/)
- Naavik's "State of UGC Games (2026)" emphasizes licensed gameplay frameworks, generative tools, and monetization/advertising tooling. I found no Naavik piece on hiring specifically. — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)
- Tracker data: Roblox Corp itself had ~400 active postings in 2026 (+39.9% vs 2025) per one tracker, but another tracker counted ~58 (Sep 29, 2026). These are corporate roles, not creator-studio roles. — [Revelio Labs](https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/roblox/employees); [Autobound](https://www.autobound.ai/companies/roblox/hiring) (conflicting)

### Inferences
- **Monetization/UI demand: mixed signals.** Falling monetization per hour gives studios a reason to want better shop/offer UX. The 18+ DevEx rate for game passes and subscriptions (prior notes) and the paid-random-item odds-disclosure rules also create specialized monetization-UI work. But I found no data showing more job posts for "UI/monetization specialists". Retention/LiveOps is the better-supported growth area.
- AI tools (Studio MCP, AI Build; see prior notes) likely squeeze low-end, simple-script commissions first. This is not verified with hiring data.

### Gaps
- No quantified demand data by role (e.g., share of Talent Hub posts by category) for 2025–26.
- No sources on demand for analytics/data roles at Roblox studios.

## 3. Pay benchmarks (USD, Robux, per-task, rev share, salary)

### Takeaway
Pay varies very widely.
- **Studio salaries (US):** roughly $55k–$105k base at Gamefam-type studios, with aggregators averaging ~$92k–$110k.
- **Freelance hourly:** about $15–40/hr mid-level, $50–120+/hr senior (vendor estimates). Upwork postings often sit at $3–35/hr.
- **DevForum/Discord:** mostly per-task in Robux. Robux pricing is worth far less in USD than it looks because of the DevEx rate.

### Cited Findings
**Studio and aggregate salaries**
- Gamefam "Programmer 3 – Luau": $65,000–$105,000 base plus variable, remote USA. A Gamefam "Programmer" listing: $55,000–$85,000. Some listings are older (one removed Oct 2024). — [Built In LA](https://www.builtinla.com/job/programmer-3-luau/3430390); [Built In: Programmer](https://builtin.com/job/programmer/2552583)
- ZipRecruiter (Oct 2026):
  - "Roblox Game Developer": average $108,471 (25th–75th percentile ~$81.5k–$124k).
  - "Roblox Developer": $109,905 (~$84k–$134.5k).
  - "Roblox Lua Programming": $92,636.
  - LA "Roblox Game Developer" (Sep 2026): $116,878.
  - These are model estimates, not verified pay. — [ZipRecruiter Game Dev](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Salaries/Roblox-Game-Developer-Salary); [ZipRecruiter Developer](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Salaries/Roblox-Developer-Salary); [ZipRecruiter Lua jobs](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Roblox-Lua-Programming)

**Revenue share**
- Typical 2025-era rev-share splits: programmer 10%, builder/modeler/animator 6%, UI 4%, SFX 3%. Older posts offer 30–40% plus 5% for staying. — [DevForum: Looking for more devs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/looking-for-more-devs/3907125)

**Upwork / Fiverr (USD)**
- Upwork Oct 2026 (prior notes): fixed budgets clustered under $200 (12 of 20). A few were $1.6k–$4.5k. Hourly ranges topped out at $15–35/hr for most. — local `research/roblox-jobs-oct2026.md`
- Upwork/Flexiple estimates: $20–50 junior, $50–100 mid, $150+ senior (aspirational). — [Flexiple](https://flexiple.com/cost-to-hire/roblox-developer) (prior notes)

**How Robux pay converts to USD**
- DevEx standard rate is $0.0038 per earned Robux ($114 per 30,000). The minimum cashout is 30,000 earned Robux and the age minimum is 13. Robux earned before Sep 5, 2025 cash out at the old $0.0035 rate, **including one-time group payouts**. A $0.0054 rate applies only to qualifying purchases by age-verified U.S. 18+ players in eligible games, not to Robux received as payment. — [Roblox DevEx docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) (snippet)
- Paying through gamepass or marketplace loses ~30% to Roblox's marketplace fee (community-reported; current official figure not confirmed here). Group payouts are the other common route. — [DevForum: Payment in talent hub](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/payment-in-talent-hub/2392274); [DevForum: Reply to issues with DevEx](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/reply-to-issues-with-devex/282766)
- A 2024 commenter: "if I was paid 1k Robux that's literally $1.50 for me" (likely after fees, taxes or a non-US context; the exact basis is unclear). — via [DevForum UI pricing search results](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-be-more-firm-with-commission-prices/3143832)

### Inferences (my calculations; not from sources)
- At $0.0038/R$:
  - 1,000 R$ ≈ $3.80
  - 5,000 R$ ≈ $19
  - 10,000 R$ ≈ $38
  - 25,000 R$ ≈ $95
  - 50,000 R$ ≈ $190
- A "5k R$ for full UI" offer is therefore ~$19 cashout value. The designer quoting $70/hr = 20K R$ is pegging Robux at the old DevEx rate ($0.0035 × 20,000 = $70). If that Robux arrives via gamepass (~30% fee), the designer nets ~14K R$ ≈ $53, unless the client grosses up. Clients, by contrast, pay retail prices for Robux (not re-verified here), so a Robux payment costs the client several times more than its DevEx cashout value. That gap makes paying in Robux inefficient for both sides.
- If the ~30% gamepass fee applies, a payer must send ~1.43× the agreed Robux for the dev to net the amount. Many rate cards add this explicitly.
- Robux pay also carries a DevEx 30k minimum, eligibility hurdles, and a hold period on earned Robux (60 days for Creator Rewards per prior notes; the hold on group payouts/sales was not verified here). Robux pay favors younger and hobbyist developers; experienced developers shift to USD (PayPal/Upwork/salary).
- Seniority bands, assembled roughly from the sources above:

| Level | Robux commission | USD freelance | US salary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hobbyist / junior | hundreds–low thousands R$ per task | $3–20/hr on Upwork | — |
| Mid | 10k–50k R$ projects | $15–40/hr | $55–85k at studios |
| Senior / lead | — | $50–120+/hr | $65–105k+ (Gamefam), ~$110k aggregate |

### Gaps
- No 2026 Talent Hub pay distribution data.
- No rate data for 2025–26 for builders, VFX artists or game designers specifically.
- No salary data for Super League, Melon, Dubit or Voldex (Voldex postings found carried no base salary in snippets).
- Retail Robux purchase price was not re-verified in this pass.

## 4. What clients and studios require; scams and non-payment risks

### Takeaway
Clients want proof of shipped work: a DevForum or Talent Hub portfolio, games with visits/CCU, and (for studios) years of Luau plus leadership experience. Vetting is light and informal. On the risk side, the main problems are unpaid rev-share or percentage deals and clients who disappear. A 2026 wave of **fake job offers delivering malware that hijacks whole games** has made "job offer" scams a serious threat to developers themselves.

### Cited Findings
- Studio requirements:
  - Voldex lead role: 3–5+ years of Roblox development and 1+ year leading 3+ people. — [startup.jobs](https://startup.jobs/lead-software-engineer-brookhaven-voldex-games-8759654)
  - Typical 2026 listings ask for Luau proficiency, shipped Roblox titles, client-server architecture, UI, retention/monetization optimization and documentation; senior roles 4+ years. — [DevForum 2026 applications](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/2026-software-engineer-game-developer-applications-now-open/4632709) (prior notes)
- Vendor guides stress multiplayer architecture, cross-device performance and LiveOps readiness. — [Innovecs](https://www.innovecsgames.com/blog/roblox-game-developers-for-hire/)
- Trial tasks are common. Upwork posts explicitly label "(Trial Project)" at $5, or a paid audit before milestones. — local `research/roblox-jobs-oct2026.md` (#6, #7, #14)
- Security advice: don't give commissioned developers Team Create access unless you fully trust them, because they could slip in malicious code. — [DevForum: How To Find Good Developers](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-find-good-developers/712311)
- **2026 game-hijacking via fake job offers:** reported by 404 Media, then Malwarebytes (June 2026) and Help Net Security (Jun 18, 2026).
  - Attackers pose as employers or companies and trick developers into running malware disguised as a job offer.
  - They take ownership of the Roblox group, move the game to a new group, and take the Robux.
  - One victim's game earned ~10,000 R$/day; recovery through Roblox support took 30+ days and moved only after media contact.
  - Roblox said no security measure can completely remove the risk when users run untrusted code.
  - [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/scams/2026/06/roblox-developers-are-losing-entire-games-to-malware-attacks); [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/06/18/roblox-game-takeover-malware-attacks/); [404 Media](https://404media.co/hackers-are-hijacking-entire-roblox-games-now)
- Non-payment anecdote: a developer reports a YouTuber client stopped responding and did not pay. The only advice in the thread was to message an admin, which shows there's little recourse. — [DevForum report](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/way-to-report-someone-who-employed-you-and-who-started-gaslightingscammingabusing-you/3881117)
- Rev-share risk: Talent Hub and DevForum posts offering percentage with no budget, or mislabelled as USD. — [DevForum: My stance on the Talent Hub](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-stance-on-the-talent-hub/2889391)
- Ownership and contract disputes escalate to courts at the high end. Example: the *Forsaken* lawsuit, in which developers say the original owner is trying to undo a transfer agreement. — [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/roblox-forsaken-lawsuit-dead-by-daylight-clone/)
- Common payment protection: 60% upfront / 40% on completion for larger jobs. Middleman/escrow services are offered by some Discord marketplaces (e.g., Developer's Spawn). — [DevForum: Elite scripter](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/elite-scripter-for-hire/3908929); [DevForum: Developer Spawn](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-developer-spawn/707385)
- Upwork red flags in the Oct 2026 sample: $5 "full game" jobs, a "recover stolen pets" job, bots, and "obtain likes" playtesting. — local `research/roblox-jobs-oct2026.md`

### Inferences
- Freelancers should insist on upfront or milestone payment, and USD where possible (PayPal/escrow/Upwork). They should treat percentage-only offers as speculative. They should never run executables or "game files" sent by prospective employers, and should use a separate account or Studio profile for client work.
- Clients increasingly gate on verifiable game links with visits/CCU, because DevForum portfolios are self-reported. Listing games you didn't actually work on is risky (see Upwork misrepresentation rules in prior notes).
- NDAs appear in studio contexts. **No source in this search** gave specifics on NDA use for freelancers.

### Gaps
- No data on how common trial tasks are, or whether they are paid, outside the Upwork sample.
- No official Roblox guidance found on developer-for-hire disputes or Talent Hub moderation.

## 5. Fiverr Roblox UI/shop/monetization gig pricing (comparison)

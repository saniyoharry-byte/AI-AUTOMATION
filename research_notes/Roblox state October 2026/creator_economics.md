## 1. DevEx rates, eligibility, payout totals, and concentration

### Takeaway
DevEx pays $0.0038 per earned Robux (since Sep 5, 2025) and $0.0054 for qualifying U.S. 18+ age-verified spend (since Jun 8, 2026). Roblox says creators earned about $1.7B via DevEx in the 12 months to Jun 30, 2026 (~+50% YoY), but quarterly DevEx growth slowed sharply to +15% in Q2 2026. Earnings are extremely concentrated: the median DevEx participant earned about $1,500, while the top 10 averaged $65.7M.

### Cited Findings
**Rates and eligibility** (see the prior notes for the 18+ rate details)
- Standard rate: $0.0038 per Robux since Sep 5, 2025 (was $0.0035); minimum cash-out is 30,000 earned Robux — [prior notes / PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-creators-will-now-earn-85-more-from-developer-exchange). Note: a Mar 2026 rolearn.dev report still says "$0.0035 per Robux", which is out of date — [rolearn.dev](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/roblox-monetization-trends-devex-creator-rewards).
- 18+ rate: $0.0054 per Robux on eligible spend from age-verified U.S. adults, effective June 8. This raises the creator's effective share of qualifying spend from 26.6% to 37.8% — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/roblox-to-raise-developer-exchange-rate-for-games-aimed-at-18-plus-players-as-adult-cohort-surges/); [Roblox docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate).
- Roblox's 10-Q defines DevEx fees as fiat payments owed to creators, based on their accumulated earned Robux — [Roblox 10-Q Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051082/rblx-20260630.htm).

Cumulative DevEx payouts since 2013 are above $5B — [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/roblox-top-creators-devex-payouts-2026-1819403); [gameaccountvalue.com](https://gameaccountvalue.com/news-roblox-2026-09-rdc-2026-creator-earnings.html); [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25918/roblox-unveils-expansions-to-play-creation-and-monetization-at-rdc).

## 2. Creator Rewards (replaced Premium Payouts / EBP on Jul 24, 2025)

### Takeaway
Creator Rewards pays two things. First, a flat 5 Robux per "Active Spender" who plays 10+ minutes per day, counted only if the game is one of the first three experiences they launch that day. Second, a 35% revenue share on the first $100 spent by new or returning users in their first two months. Payouts are uncapped and held 60 days. No 2026 rate change was confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Daily engagement reward: "creators will earn 5 Robux when an Active Spender spends at least 10 minutes inside their experience throughout a day, provided it's one of the first three experiences they launch that day." Roblox notes the rate may change — [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628).
- Active Spender: someone who has spent at least $9.99 on Roblox in the last 60 days and did not sign up or reactivate in that window — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628).
- Audience Expansion (replaced the Creator Affiliate program): 35% revenue share on the first $100 of Robux purchases by new or returning users (inactive 60+ days) during their first two months on the platform — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628). A third-party guide says it requires an ID-verified creator and a valid DevEx account — [rolearn.dev guide](https://rolearn.dev/insights/roblox-creator-rewards-payout-guide).
- Hold and cap: earned Robux are added after a 60-day holding period, and Creator Rewards payouts are not capped — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628); [Creator Rewards docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/creator-rewards.md).
- The Creator Rewards docs page shows an update date of June 17, 2026, but the snippet did not reveal what changed — [Creator Rewards docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/creator-rewards.md).
- Roblox's SEC filings confirm the replacement date and that rewards are based on "achievement of various metrics" — [10-Q Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm).
- RDC 2026: discovery rankings will use data from the first 28 days after launch, not just the first week. This affects how quickly new games build the audiences Creator Rewards pays on — [gameaccountvalue.com](https://gameaccountvalue.com/news-roblox-2026-09-rdc-2026-creator-earnings.html).

### Inferences
- The program rewards being in a high-value player's first three launches of the day, which means daily-return habit loops (daily rewards, streaks, live events). Long sessions do not add pay: one guide claims "30-min sessions earn 2–3x more", but that contradicts the flat 10-minute threshold and is unverified ([obby.fun](https://www.obby.fun/blog/roblox-creator-rewards-guide)).
- Audience Expansion pays games that bring new or lapsed players to Roblox, for example through off-platform marketing and the coming standalone apps from "Roblox Everywhere".

### Gaps
- Not found: 2026 changes to the 5-Robux rate, to the 35% share, or to the Active Spender definition. The docs could not be fetched to check the Jun 17, 2026 revision.
- Not found: the Creator Rewards share of total DevEx.

## 3. In-experience monetization tools in 2026

### Takeaway
The toolkit is: passes and developer products, with optional Managed Pricing (automated price testing plus regional prices); experience subscriptions; rewarded video ads, open to all eligible creators since Feb 2026; Commerce APIs for physical goods (Shopify); and Roblox Plus incentives, launched Apr 30, 2026, which pay creators for Plus sign-ups and private-server use.

### Cited Findings
**Passes and developer products / fees**
- Roblox's beginner guidance says a Game Pass plus a Developer Product is usually enough for a first game. Subscriptions, paid access and private servers can be added later (third-party guide) — [RTS School](https://www.rts.school/en/blog/how-to-monetize-a-roblox-game).
- Fees background (pre-2026 docs, not re-verified this session): creators keep about 70% of Robux from pass and developer product sales after Roblox's marketplace fee. That fee is the basis of the "26.6% effective share" figure in Finding 1 (0.7 × DevEx value of Robux bought) — [Roblox monetization docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization). No 2026 change to the marketplace fee was found.

**Managed Pricing (price optimization + regional pricing)**
- Creator Hub combines price optimization (automated tests that find price points for passes and developer products) and regional pricing into one opt-in feature called Managed Pricing — [Managed Pricing docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/managed-pricing).
- Regional prices are set from purchasing power, exchange rates and local spending behavior, and stay within 30%–100% of the default price. Creators choose which items are priced regionally. Items below the experiment-eligibility threshold are not affected by price optimization — [Managed Pricing docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/managed-pricing).
- Regional pricing launched in April 2025. In early tests the share of users spending rose about 17% in Mexico, 26% in Brazil and 52% in the Philippines — [Roblox newsroom (Apr 2025)](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/04/roblox-launches-regional-pricing-for-in-experience-items).
- Unverified third-party claim of "30–80% increases in international revenue" — [rolearn.dev](https://rolearn.dev/guidance/regional-pricing-international-markets).

**Experience subscriptions** (older; launched 2024–25)
- Eligible, verified creators create subscriptions in Creator Hub and add them with Studio APIs. Subscribers pay in real-world currency so renewals are automatic — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/roblox-is-launching-subscriptions-for-eligible-creators/). Subscriptions count as spend eligible for the 18+ DevEx rate — [prior notes / Roblox docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate).

**Rewarded video ads**
- Roblox expanded access to all creators who meet its Advertising and Community Standards, regardless of maturity rating. Earnings = EPM (earnings per 1,000 impressions) × impressions. Ads are shown only to users 13+. Violations can lead to revoked or suspended payouts — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/more-creators-can-now-use-rewarded-video-ads/3838678). A secondary site dates the rollout to all eligible creators as Feb 18, 2026 — [creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/learn/how-to-make-money-on-roblox-2026).
- Q1 2026 shareholder letter: Roblox says rewarded video has product-market fit and announced a plugin template to make it easier to integrate, plus an option for advertisers to optimize for ROI or play count — [Q1 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm).
- Background: a Google partnership on rewarded video was announced in 2025 — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/roblox-expands-google-advertising-partnership-with-rewarded-video-launch/). Morgan Stanley estimated rewarded video could reach $3B by 2027 — [Sherwood](https://www.sherwood.news/markets/robloxs-new-google-advertising-partnership-could-give-investors-1-2-billion/).
- Unverified claims: creators report Roblox keeps about 70% of ad revenue; a developer reportedly said Brookhaven earns about $65K/month from ads; trend sites say ads are 5–15% of revenue in games that use them well — [search summary of creation.dev / rolearn.dev](https://rolearn.dev/insights/rewarded-video-ads-new-revenue-stream). Not confirmed by Roblox.
- RDC 2026: creators can already add monetization from the Creator Hub mobile app, and launching ads for their own games from mobile is "coming soon" — [gameaccountvalue.com](https://gameaccountvalue.com/news-roblox-2026-09-rdc-2026-creator-earnings.html).

**Commerce / real-world products** (older; launched May 2025)
- The Commerce APIs let eligible creators sell physical products inside their experiences, with Shopify as the first partner. Launch was limited to all-ages experiences and U.S. users aged 13+ — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/15/roblox-now-lets-creators-sell-physical-products-within-their-experiences); [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/05/roblox-launches-shopify-integration-amp-licensing-program).
- Approved Merchandiser Program (AMP): physical products come with a code for a matching digital item. Example: about 90% of Twin Atlas's orders came through in-game commerce, and half of those were from repeat customers — [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/05/roblox-launches-shopify-integration-amp-licensing-program).
- No 2026 expansion to more markets or partners was found.

**Roblox Plus** (launched Apr 30, 2026; $4.99/mo; replaced new Premium sign-ups)
- Roblox covers Plus subscribers' discounts, so creators still earn the same per item — [TubeFilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/13/roblox-plus-creator-payouts); [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/en-au/newsroom/2026/04/introducing-roblox-plus-subscription).
- Sign-up bonus: 250 Robux per month for each of a new subscriber's first three paid months (up to 750 Robux), when they subscribe through `PromptRobloxSubscriptionPurchase` in the creator's game. Free trials don't count, and there is a 60-day hold — [Roblox Plus docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/roblox-plus); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-plus/4567894/1).
- Private-server bonus: up to 100 Robux per Plus subscriber who spends 60+ minutes in a creator's paid private servers. Wording differs: the launch post says per 30 days, the docs say monthly. Payouts cover at most the 5 private servers where each user spent the most time — [Roblox Plus docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/roblox-plus); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-plus/4567894/1).
- The 10% bonus Robux for Premium subscribers ended May 30, 2026. Existing Premium members keep their stipend — [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/en-au/newsroom/2026/04/introducing-roblox-plus-subscription); PCGamesN's account conflicts — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/plus-subscription).
- Plus incentives paid creators more than 300M Robux in their first four months, through Aug 17, 2026 (RDC 2026) — [gameaccountvalue.com](https://gameaccountvalue.com/news-roblox-2026-09-rdc-2026-creator-earnings.html). That is about $1.1M at $0.0038 (inference).
- Plus is required for creators who want their games in the Roblox Kids/Select catalogs (see prior notes).

### Inferences
- Paid private servers became more valuable in 2026: they earn the 18+ DevEx rate and the Plus private-server bonus. Any game with a social or grind loop should offer them.
- Integrating `PromptRobloxSubscriptionPurchase` is low effort with a clear payoff (up to 750 Robux per conversion). Expect it to become a standard item on dev-for-hire job lists.
- For games under the price-test eligibility threshold, regional pricing is the main lever available.

### Gaps
- Pages for paid access, avatar-item, and UGC marketplace fees could not be fetched to confirm the 2026 fee percentages.
- No official 2026 rewarded-video EPM figures or revenue split were found.
- No "Immersive Ads" status for 2026 was found. They are believed deprecated in favor of rewarded video, but this is unverified.

## 4. Best-practice monetization patterns (top games, Roblox guidance)

### Takeaway
Roblox's official guidance favors immediate-value purchases and warns that appointment and timer mechanics are unpopular. Top games rely on passes (permanent perks), consumable developer products (luck boosts, currency, skips), cosmetic skins and battle passes (e.g., Rivals), and private servers. Paid RNG must disclose odds, and child-protection research is increasing scrutiny of these mechanics.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox docs: "appointment mechanics and timers which can be removed or brought forward may work well on other platforms, but these mechanics are unpopular with Roblox users." Players split into "tourists", who hop between experiences and prefer immediate-effect items, and "locals", who form almost all of the engaged base. Roblox also notes that monetization is not guaranteed — [Roblox monetization docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization).
- Rivals is reported to monetize almost entirely through weapon skins and battle passes. Estimated monthly revenue: Rivals $8–15M+, Blox Fruits $8–14M, Brookhaven RP $6–12M, Adopt Me! $5–10M. These are third-party estimates; Roblox does not disclose per-game revenue — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform).
- A University of Sydney study (Mar 2026) of 15 top-earning Roblox games documented 14 "misleading, deceptive or unfair" monetization practices — [USyd repository](https://ses.library.usyd.edu.au/handle/2123/35033).
- Paid random items, including luck boosts and pity systems, require full odds disclosure (see prior notes) — [Roblox docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items).
- Q2 2026 earnings: engagement shifted from high-monetizing 2025 viral games to games with lower monetization per hour (see prior notes) — [Q2 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm).

### Inferences
- Common pattern set for 2026, synthesized from the above and the incentive structure rather than one source:
  - Tiered passes: 2x currency, VIP, extra slots.
  - Consumable developer products: luck and boost timers, currency packs, revives/skips.
  - A cheap starter pack early for tourists.
  - Seasonal battle or season pass for locals.
  - Limited-time cosmetics.
  - Paid private servers, now boosted by the Plus bonus and the 18+ rate.
  - In-game Plus sign-up prompt.
  - Opt-in rewarded video for free players.
  - Managed Pricing turned on.
- Design paid RNG to be "odds-disclosure ready", and avoid dark patterns flagged in the USyd study, especially for games aiming at Kids/Select catalogs.

### Gaps
- No official Roblox guidance on starter-pack or battle-pass pricing was found, and no 2026 Naavik or Deconstructor of Fun deep-dive on Roblox monetization surfaced in search.
- Pass vs product vs ad revenue mix for top games is not publicly disclosed.

## 5. Revenue distribution / concentration

### Takeaway
The top 1,000 creators averaged about $1.4M each in the 12 months to Jun 2026. The median of 42K+ DevEx participants made about $1.5K, and DevEx participants are themselves only a small subset of all creators. Concentration at the very top grew year over year: the top-10 average roughly doubled from $33.9M to $65.7M.

### Cited Findings
- Same RDC 2026 figures as Finding 1 — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113585/robloxs-top-10-creators-averaged-dollars65-7-million-each-while-the-median-made-dollars1500-and-roblox-everywhere-is-the-fix/index.html); [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/roblox-top-creators-devex-payouts-2026-1819403).
- IBTimes' headline frames the top 10 as "viral games built in months" — [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/roblox-top-creators-devex-payouts-2026-1819403).

### Inferences
- The top 1,000 likely take about 80% of DevEx dollars (see the math in Finding 1). For a freelancer, client revenue is bimodal: a few studios can pay well, while most game owners earn little and favor revenue-share or low-budget deals.

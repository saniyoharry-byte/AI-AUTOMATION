## 1. Which additional niches do 2025–2026 analyses flag as fast-growing or under-served?

### Takeaway
Beyond the niches in the first study, the strongest *published* signals come from Fiverr's search indexes and Upwork releases:
- AI agent deployment
- Claude Code / AI-coding specialists
- Go High Level and Make.com setup
- Newsletter platforms (Substack, Beehiiv)
- Faceless YouTube production
- Prompt engineering
- QA testing (Upwork monthly headline)
- AI governance/compliance (non-Upwork data)

### Inferences
- **[Inference]** The best-evidenced *new* niches are AI-agent building and deployment, **Claude Code / AI-coding specialist** work, and CRM/marketing-automation platform setup (Go High Level, Make). They suit a CS graduate, but they are search-growth signals from Fiverr, not Upwork hire data. They overlap partly with the "AI automation" niche in the first study, so position them as *platform-specific* (for example, "Go High Level + WhatsApp automations for clinics" or "Claude Code agent builds").
- **[Inference]** MCP server work is best treated as a sub-skill inside AI-agent/integration gigs ("connect your CRM/DB to Claude"), not as a standalone niche. Upwork's own MCP launch shows the protocol has mainstream traction, but there is no evidence of a standalone job pool.
- **[Inference]** AI governance/compliance documentation pays well but is credibility-gated (legal/regulatory trust). For a newcomer it is realistic only as narrow deliverables, such as AI-use policies, AI Act disclosure checklists or model cards for SMBs, after building a portfolio.
- **[Inference]** QA testing (including AI-agent/LLM output testing) is a low-barrier, CS-friendly entry. Upwork flagged rising QA demand in Nov 2025. Game QA looks crowded on supply.

## 2. African-language AI data work (Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin): demand, rates, eligibility, scams

### Takeaway
There is real, funded demand for African-language *datasets* (Masakhane, African Next Voices, Lelapa AI). Paid freelance task work exists, but rates are modest. The one direct listing found is **RWS TrainAI Yoruba at $8 (USD)**. A removed 2024 Outlier Yoruba listing averaged $15/hr. **Eligibility for Nigerians is shifting and contested.** Outlier reportedly removed Nigeria in July 2026, and DataAnnotation.tech appears closed to Nigeria. Mindrift, Micro1, CrowdGen (Appen), Mercor, Alignerr and Prolific are reported as open. Scam risk centres on WhatsApp "agents" charging registration fees.

### Cited Findings
**Demand side (projects and labs)**
- **Masakhane:**
  - More than 2,000 NLP researchers.
  - Launched an African Languages Hub in April 2025 (goal: AI tools for 1 billion Africans by 2029).
  - In January 2026, started building datasets for **50 languages**, including speech data for 18, funded by Google.org, FCDO, IDRC and Gates.

  [Press] — [Dawan Africa](https://www.dawan.africa/news/masakhane-africas-language-focused-hub-aims-to-integrate-african-languages-into-ai)
- **African Next Voices** (Gates Foundation, with Meta support) released what is described as the largest African-language AI dataset so far. It has sites in Kenya, **Nigeria** and South Africa. [Press/academic] — [NTU / The Conversation, Oct 2025](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cas/news-events/news/detail/african-languages-for-ai--the-project-that-s-gathering-a-huge-new-dataset); [ModernGhana](https://www.modernghana.com/news/1441123/african-languages-for-ai-the-project-thats-gathe.html)

### Inferences
- **[Inference]** African-language work is a real but **low-volume, low-to-mid pay ($8–15/hr) side channel**, not a main business. Yoruba has the most visible demand. Igbo, Hausa and Pidgin postings are scarcer. The bigger upside for a CS graduate is technical AI-training work (code evaluation, STEM reasoning), which aggregators price higher, combined with language skills as a differentiator.
- **[Inference]** Because eligibility changes month to month, the user should check each platform's official country list directly and never pay to join. Mercor, Mindrift, Micro1 and Appen CrowdGen look like the most reliable currently open routes. Masakhane and African Next Voices are worth monitoring for paid data-collection calls.
- **[Inference]** On Upwork itself, a profile combining "Yoruba/Igbo/Hausa/Pidgin + English" for **AI translation post-editing, LLM evaluation and transcription** fits Upwork's stated demand for "humanizing" AI translation (see the prior file).

- No pay data for Hausa or Pidgin specifically, and no data on whether Masakhane or African Next Voices contributors are paid, or how much.
- I could not resolve Outlier's Nigeria eligibility because the two blogs conflict and the official page was not fetched.
- No Toloka, TELUS or Scale/Remotasks Nigeria-specific 2026 data was found.

## 3. Structural advantages for Nigerian freelancers

### Takeaway
The evidenced advantages are **time-zone fit with the UK/EU** (same as UK time in winter, one hour ahead in summer) and **local payments knowledge** (Paystack/Flutterwave/Monnify). There are existing Upwork and Fiverr gigs for the payments work, though they are cheap ($35–50). English fluency and African-language skill feed AI-training and post-editing work. I found no quantified data on African market research demand.

### Cited Findings
- A UK IT leader says Nigeria offers "better quality… at a comparable cost, with a better timezone" than a large Indian team with poor overlap. Nigeria's time zone sits close to European capitals, yet the country is "too often overlooked". [Press, anecdote] — [The Stack](https://www.thestack.technology/nigerian-tech-talent/)
- Nigeria stays on UTC+1 all year. It matches UK time in winter and is one hour ahead during British Summer Time. [Low-quality pages; this is a basic, well-known fact] — search snippet (unreliable pages, not cited as authority)
- Paystack/Flutterwave/Monnify integration gigs exist on Upwork ($35–50) and Fiverr, often from Nigerian sellers. Paystack's API is considered easier to work with. — [Upwork catalog gig](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/development-it-fluterwave-paystack-monnify-payment-gateway-integration-into-your-website-1613678855354716160); [TechEconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/paystack-vs-flutterwave-two-strategies-one-problem/)
- The prior file already covers Nigeria's ICT share of GDP and the Bookipi pay ranking.

### Inferences
- **[Inference]** Niches with a structural Nigerian edge:
  1. **African-market payments and fintech integration**, plus WhatsApp Business API commerce bots. WhatsApp is the dominant channel in Nigeria and the UK, and local developers know Paystack, Flutterwave and Monnify KYC flows. Clients are African startups and diaspora or UK firms selling into Africa.
  2. **UK/EU-hours roles**: support engineering, QA and AI-agent monitoring/ops.
  3. **African-language AI evaluation and post-editing**.
  4. **Africa go-to-market research** for foreign SaaS and fintech companies. This one is unevidenced.
- **[Inference]** The Paystack/Flutterwave pool is small and price-compressed ($35–50 gigs). Position it as part of a broader "Stripe + Paystack + Flutterwave billing / subscription / webhook" offer aimed at startups that sell in both Africa and the West.

### Gaps
- No Techpoint or Nairametrics 2025–2026 data on Nigerian freelancers' category mix or UK/EU client share.
- No data quantifying demand for "African market research" freelance work.
- Nigeria-specific Upwork payout and KYC frictions in 2026 were not researched.

**Date:** 2026-10-30. **Source:** Upwork MCP `find_freelancers`, which was used only to search and to read profiles.
**Prepared for:** Harry (Nigeria, 0 jobs, title "Short-Form Video Editor | Reels, TikTok & YouTube Shorts | Podcasts", $10/hr).

> Rules: every number below comes from the API response. Anything marked **(inference)** is my reading of that data. The API shows at most 10 contracts per profile, so some "first contract" entries are only the *earliest visible* contract. Each case says so.
> The API did not return review text for every contract. Many contracts show no feedback because they are still open.

## Method

- **Searches (8):**
  - `rising_talent=true`, limit 25: "short form video editor", "podcast clips editor".
  - `rising_talent=true`, limit 10: "Reels editor", "TikTok editor". I used a lower limit for these two to keep the output small, and most of their results duplicated other searches.
  - `rising_talent=true` + `country=Nigeria`, limit 25: "short form video editor", "video editor".
  - `country=Nigeria` + `earnings_max=3000`, limit 25: "short form video editor".
- **Profile reads (12):** 6 Nigerian and 6 from other countries. I chose short-form editors with earnings of $300+ to $2K+ and no Top Rated badge.
- **Result:** all 12 have contracts that started in Sep–Oct 2026, so all 12 count as "recent orders". The target was 10.
- **Caveat:** several of these accounts are not brand new by job count. Qosim has 18 jobs, Elizabeth 21, Oyinkansola 35 and Isaac 40, but each has earned only $1K–$2K.
- **Freshest accounts:** Chioma (1 job), Farida (2 jobs), Mohamed (6 jobs), Lam (12 jobs) and Linh (12 jobs) are the closest to Harry's position.

**First-order pattern (inference from the data above):**
- The entry point was almost always a **single reel or short edit at a $5–$30 fixed price**, often closed within 1–3 days with a 5★ review. 9/12 have a ≤ $20 fixed video contract in their visible history.
- Several of these were explicitly called a **test or trial**.
- Within 2–6 weeks, at least 6 of the 12 turned a small job into a larger hourly or repeat contract ($111–$360): Linh, Dua, Areej, Lam, Alexandro, Farida.
- A video intro and a consultation listing were **not** required: Chioma, Farida, Mohamed, Areej, Lam and Alexandro had no intro.

## Suggested actions for Harry (derived from the checklist)

1. **Add 6–9 short-form video portfolio items and move them to the top.** Mohamed's numbered series format ("01/02/03 — TALKING HEAD & PODCASTS") or before/after reels like Dua's work well. Spec edits of public podcast clips, as Farida and Lam did, are a common way to fill this. Hide or move the web, AI and Roblox items below them.
2. **Add 1–2 employment entries for video editing**, such as freelance or self-employed short-form editor, as Isaac and Mohamed have. Only list what is true. Avoid copying the 75–100-row employment lists; they look unverifiable **(inference)**.
3. **Start sending video proposals now**, aimed at small single-reel or "test video" jobs ($10–$30 fixed). That is where 9/12 started.
4. Optional: add a 30–60 second video intro (5/12 have one) and a $10–$20 consultation (4/12).
5. Keep the profile text as it is. Harry's title, rate, skills and overview already match or exceed these profiles. Adding one verifiable social-proof line after the first job would help.

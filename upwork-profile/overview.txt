🧭 HOW I WORK: THE LAUNCH-READY PROCESS

1. Scope. I study your idea or your game, then send a written plan: features, milestones, timeline and price. If something does not fit your budget, I tell you before we start, never halfway through.
2. Playable prototype first. You play the core loop in Roblox before I build content around it.
3. Build in milestones. Short updates with video clips so you always see real progress.
4. Test like a player. Multiplayer tests, PC and mobile checks, exploit checks, and no new errors in the output.
5. Launch-ready polish. Performance tuning for low-end phones, UI cleanup, and help with your icon, thumbnails and game page.
6. Handover and LiveOps. Clean, commented, modular Luau and a short written guide. I stay on for weekly updates, events and new features.

## 2. Other experience: 20 entries

Paste each one into **Profile → Other experiences → Add**: the bold line goes in "Subject", the paragraph in "Description". Entries 1–3 are the ones I gave you before. Add them first if they aren't on your profile yet.

**1. Podcast & Long-Form to Short-Form Repurposing**
I turn long podcasts, interviews, webinars and YouTube videos into short clips for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. I watch the full recording and pick the moments that work on their own: a strong opinion, a story, a useful tip or a funny reaction. Each clip gets a hook in the first 3 seconds, clean cuts that remove pauses and filler words, animated captions and a clear ending. One episode usually gives 8–20 clips, delivered in 9:16 with titles and caption ideas.

**2. Hooks, Captions & Retention Editing for Reels, TikTok and Shorts**
Short-form video is won or lost in the first 3 seconds. I edit for retention: I open with the strongest line or visual, cut every dead second, add pattern interrupts (zooms, B-roll, sound effects, text pops) every few seconds, and use word-by-word animated captions that work with the sound off. I follow each platform's safe zones and check every video on a phone before delivery.

**3. AI-Assisted Video Workflow (Opus Clip, ElevenLabs, Higgsfield, Kling, Veo)**
I use AI tools to work faster and add value, not to replace editing. Opus Clip helps me scan long videos for possible moments, which I then review and cut by hand. ElevenLabs creates clean voiceovers. Higgsfield, Kling and Veo generate AI B-roll and product scenes when there is not enough real footage. Every final video is edited in Premiere Pro or CapCut and checked by me.

**4. Weekly Short-Form Clip Pipeline Management**
I manage the full short-form pipeline for creators and businesses, from raw recording to ready-to-post files. I own the weekly delivery schedule, so clients never miss a posting day. I coordinate through one shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder: clients drop new recordings, and I return finished clips in labelled batches. I keep a simple clip log (Google Sheets) of what was delivered, posted and planned next, so nothing gets lost and the client approves in minutes.

**5. Talking-Head Video Editing for Coaches, Consultants & Founders**
I edit talking-head videos, where one person speaks to camera, into tight, engaging Reels, TikToks and Shorts. I cut pauses, mistakes and filler words, add punch-in zooms on key points, bold captions with highlighted keywords, B-roll that shows what is being said, and light sound design. The goal is a video that feels fast and confident even when the raw footage was one long, unedited take.

**6. Finding the Best Moments: Clip Selection from Long Videos**
Many clients have hours of content and no time to rewatch it. I take that job: I review full episodes or recordings, note every strong moment with a timestamp, and rank them by how well they work alone as a short clip. I send the client a short list of the best moments with suggested hooks, so they can approve, skip or add ideas before I edit.

**7. Brand Templates & Consistent Editing Style**
I build a reusable template for each client: caption style, fonts, colours, logo, intro or outro cards, lower thirds and progress bars. I save it as Premiere Pro Essential Graphics or MOGRT templates (or CapCut templates), so every clip looks like the same brand. When a client sends reference videos, I match the pacing, text style and music feel, then keep it the same week after week.

**8. Multi-Camera Podcast Editing (Riverside, Zoom, Descript)**
I edit multi-camera and remote podcast recordings from Riverside, Zoom and similar tools. I sync the speakers, switch to the person talking, clean up cross-talk and long pauses, and use Descript for fast text-based rough cuts when a client wants full episodes. From the same recording I then cut vertical clips with the speaker's face kept centred in 9:16.

**9. YouTube Shorts Repurposing from Long-Form YouTube Videos**
I turn horizontal YouTube videos into vertical YouTube Shorts. I reframe 16:9 footage to 9:16 so the speaker or action stays centred, pick the strongest 30–60 seconds, add a title bar, captions and a loop-friendly ending that encourages replays. This lets YouTubers grow with Shorts from content they have already published.

**10. UGC & Direct-Response Video Ad Editing (Meta, TikTok)**
I edit UGC-style and product footage into 15–30 second ads for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Each ad opens with a scroll-stopping hook, uses on-screen text that works with the sound off, product close-ups, B-roll and a clear call to action. I deliver several hook versions per ad (question, bold claim, problem/solution), so brands can test which one performs best.

**11. AI B-roll, Product Scenes & AI Video Ads**
When a brand has little or no footage, I create it. I generate product shots, lifestyle scenes and B-roll with Higgsfield, Kling and Veo, add an ElevenLabs voiceover, then edit everything in Premiere Pro or CapCut with captions, pacing and music. The result is a finished ad or social video that doesn't look like generic AI content.

**12. Audio Cleanup, Voiceover & Loudness for Social Video**
Bad audio makes viewers scroll away. I clean up noisy podcast and phone recordings: noise reduction, de-essing, levelling between speakers and removing echo where possible. I normalise voice to around -14 LUFS for social platforms and mix background music low (around -25 dB) so speech stays clear. When a script needs a voice, I produce AI voiceovers with ElevenLabs.

**13. Sound Design & Music for Short-Form Retention**
I use sound to keep people watching: whooshes on cuts, pops on text, risers before key points and music that matches the energy of the clip. I pick licensed or royalty-free tracks the client can safely post, and I time cuts to the beat when the style calls for it. Good sound design makes a simple talking-head video feel professional.

**14. Colour Correction & Grading for Phone and Webcam Footage (DaVinci Resolve)**
Most creator footage is shot on phones or webcams with uneven light. I correct exposure, white balance and skin tones, match colour between cameras and scenes, and apply a consistent look that fits the client's brand. I use DaVinci Resolve for grading and Premiere Pro Lumetri for quick fixes, so every clip in a series looks the same.

**15. Motion Graphics & Animated Text (After Effects)**
I create motion graphics that make short-form content clearer and more engaging: animated titles, keyword highlights, emoji and icon pops, simple charts, call-out arrows, lower thirds and progress bars. I build them in After Effects, or as reusable MOGRT templates in Premiere Pro, so they can be applied quickly across every clip in a client's series.

**16. Multi-Format Exports & Platform Safe Zones (9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9)**
I deliver each video in the sizes a client needs: 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, 1:1 and 4:5 for feed posts and ads, and 16:9 for YouTube. I keep captions, faces and calls to action inside each platform's safe zones, so nothing is hidden by buttons or descriptions, and I export with the right settings for quality and file size.

**17. Faceless Short-Form Videos (Script, AI Voiceover & B-roll)**
I create faceless short-form videos for channels that don't want to film: a short script or fact list, an ElevenLabs voiceover, and stock or AI-generated B-roll with captions and music. Each video opens with a strong question or claim and changes visuals every few seconds to hold attention. Ideal for faceless YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram pages that need consistent posting.

**18. Fast-Turnaround Batch Editing (24–48 Hours)**
Clients who post daily need edits fast and reliably. I work in batches: once the style and template are approved, I deliver the first clip within 24–48 hours and full weekly batches on a fixed day. I keep organised project files and can share editable Premiere Pro, CapCut, DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro projects on request, so the client always owns their work.

**19. Clip Titles, Captions & Posting Support**
Along with each clip, I write a short on-screen title and a posting caption idea with relevant hashtags, so the client can post in seconds. When a client wants it, I can also upload or schedule clips to their channels using the access they give me, or organise finished files by platform and date in their shared folder.

**20. Revisions, Feedback & Client Communication**
I make feedback easy: clients can comment with timestamps, and I apply clear, specific revisions (up to 2 rounds per clip in my standard plans). I keep communication on Upwork, reply quickly from Lagos (GMT+1, overlapping UK/EU hours and US mornings) and confirm deadlines in writing, so clients always know when their next batch will arrive.

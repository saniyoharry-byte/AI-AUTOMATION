## 3. What clients ask for

### Genres and the games clients name
- **The "Steal a …" base-stealing format** (Steal a Brainrot lineage): #37 "Steal a Bacon" has 8 bases, stealing, a base LOCK, rebirth, a bat with ragdoll, and purchase announcements. #6's mystery-crate ASMR simulator with bases, workers and offline earnings follows the same loop.
- **Tycoon / simulator / clicker:** #7 (tycoon with droppers and conveyors), #30, #6. #18 wants a remake of **Airplane Factory Simulator** (by rayraydog).
- **Obby:** #5, and #32's **Halloween** 100-stage obby with a secret Stage 101. Seasonal content is new in this sample.
- **Tower defense / RTS:** #36 (a live RTS + TD with heroes and a ranked ladder). #25 is a co-op build-and-defend survival game with an "adaptive enemy".
- **RPG:** #10 (NPCs and bosses with hitboxes) and #21 WildBound, a creature-collecting open world. In effect a Pokémon-style game, though Pokémon is mentioned only as an IP to avoid.
- **City roleplay:** #13 "City Live" with police, crime, jobs, vehicles and an economy.
- **Stealth / kids:** "Hall Monitor: School Candy Heist" (#22 maps, #26 skins). Players are 8–14 and mostly on phones.
- **Named live games:** **Wash a House** (#34, 2,500–5,000 CCU), Hall Monitor, Airplane Factory Simulator.
- **Not seen this time:** Grow a Garden, Dress to Impress, +1 Speed.

### Systems requested in dev jobs
- **DataStore saving:** 7 jobs (#5, #6, #7, #30, #37, #38, plus the GDD job)
- **Rebirth / prestige:** 3 (#6, #7, #37)
- **Gamepasses / developer products / shop GUI:** #7, #30, #37, #38 and #25
- **Mobile + PC UI:** #5, #6, #7, #30
- **Inventory, placement and crates:** #6
- Also: checkpoints, respawn and leaderboards (#5); RemoteEvents, server security and exploit prevention (#6, #38); base ownership and locks, ragdoll and melee (#37); NPC AI, hitboxes and move sets (#10); workers and offline earnings (#6); multiplayer testing with 4–8 players.

### Tools
- **Dev:** Roblox Studio and Luau in every dev job
- **Animation:** Moon Animator / Moon Animator 2 (#2, #20, #35) and Blender (most animation and 3D jobs)
- **3D:** Maya (#8), Photoshop (2D clothing, thumbnails)
- **Video:** Premiere, After Effects and CapCut
- **AI tools:** ElevenLabs for voice (#37) and AI voiceovers for shorts (#12, #27)

### Exact phrases that recur
- "Works on mobile and PC" / "mobile-friendly UI" / "smooth on both PC and mobile"
- "The current code is a bit messy, so please don't break what already works" (#7). Variants: "audit, repair, and finish", "not starting from zero".
- "No AI-generated work / animations", "not ai" (#1, #2, #3). "All designs must be original" / "No copied, stolen, or unlicensed systems" (#6, #26).
- "Serious applicants only… No time-wasters" (#2), "IF YOU ARE NOT HAPPY WITH ANY OF THESE REQUIREMENTS DO NOT APPLY" (#20)
- "You must be confident that you can match the style… I won't compromise on quality" (#2, #35, #40)
- "Long-term work / ongoing work for the right person", "trial project / test animation"
- **Security and ownership:** "I will NOT give you my Roblox password" and "No hidden backdoors, unauthorized admin accounts, ownership changes" (#37). "Work only in a duplicate testing version and never modify or publish the original game without permission" (#6). "I publish from my own Roblox account; you deliver the files only" (#23). "I will retain ownership of the game and its revenue… full project handover, including source scripts" (#30, #13).

### What applicants are asked to include
- **Code words:** start the proposal with **"POTATO"** (#27) or **"OBBY"** (#38).
- **Links to published Roblox games you personally scripted, and which part you built:** #6, #30, #33, #38, #13.
- **Numbered questions to answer:** #6 has 6 (audit approach, price for the audit, beta timeline). #8 has 5 (asset categories, what you personally handled, validation steps, rates).
- **UGC proof:** Marketplace links plus wireframes, cages, rigging and bake screenshots (#8); 2–3 examples of bodies or dynamic heads and confirmation of a 3-day delivery (#23).
- **Shorts:** portfolio, rate per video, videos per week, turnaround and availability (#2, #14, #35). Paid tests: a 5–7 s test animation (#2), a 10 s test at $1/s (#40), one trial video (#14).
- **Promotion:** view and engagement results, and whether you post on your own accounts (#32).
- **Dev work:** a short note on what you changed, delivered with the tested build (#7).

| Category | Earlier sample (50 jobs) | Now (40 jobs) | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Game dev | 19 (38%) | 15 (38%) | Steady share |
| Animation for shorts | 11 (22%) | 9 (22%) | Steady |

| Other + testing | 5 (10%) | 6 (15%) | Up; new kinds of request (AI prompt, likes, bot, account recovery) |

- **Trend games:** Grow a Garden, Dress to Impress and +1 Speed have dropped out. Steal a Brainrot survives as a clone ("Steal a Bacon", using the Bacon Hair meme). Tower defense continues (RTS + TD critique, co-op defense GDD).
- **New themes:** Halloween seasonal obby; ASMR / mystery-crate simulator; creature collector; stealth game for kids; city RP; and **"finish or repair an existing game"** briefs (#5, #6, #7, #33).
- **Fear of backdoors and passwords:** still present but less dominant. One explicit "no backdoors / no password" clause (#37). Ownership and sandbox clauses appear in #6, #23 and #30, and #38 asks about exploit prevention.
- **New concerns:** verifying that applicants **personally** did the work ("explain exactly what you personally handled") and rejecting AI-generated work.
- **Prices:** the low end has fallen further ($5 trial tasks; $1/s animation). The serious dev jobs ($2k for the ASMR game, $4.5k for UGC) want proven, published games.

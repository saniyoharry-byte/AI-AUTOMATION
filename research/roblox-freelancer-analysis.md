Research date: October 2026. 50 profiles selected from Upwork search (Roblox, earnings > $0): 20 Nigeria, 30 other countries, 14+ with no badge. 48 were readable (Manish S. and Azamat U. are private). Full per-profile notes, every visible contract and client reviews: `freelancers/batch1.md` to `batch5.md`.

Data limits: Upwork's API does not return Job Success Score from profiles, response time or total hours; several profiles show only their 10 most recent contracts. "Why they were hired" is inferred from the job title, the contract size, the client's review and the freelancer's profile at the time of research.

## 5. Why these freelancers get invites and messages

1. **Roblox-titled job history and reviews.** Upwork's search and "Best Match" lean on completed jobs that match the client's job. The big earners have dozens of Roblox contract titles.
2. **Reviews that praise communication.** Almost every 5★ review mentions communication, responsiveness or clarifying questions. Not one praises code quality alone.
3. **Proof the client can check**: named games with links, visits or CCU numbers (Abdullah, Shivam, Harshit, Hoang), and video demos (Jay V.).
4. **Outcome language**: retention, monetization, "games that make money" (Harshit, Dilpreet, Shivam).
5. **Low-risk entry points**: paid consultations ($35–75), small trials, Project Catalog packages.
6. **A clear niche or buyer**: maps (Oma), UGC (Oloruntoba), economy design (Ali Dogan), tutoring for parents (Dexter, Andrew), Roblox + web APIs (Avirup).
7. **Team backing** for big builds (Dilpreet, Mayank, Harshit via ChicMic).
8. **Location and English** for premium work: US freelancers charge $40–65 for tutoring and builds.

## 6. What will make Harry start getting invites and messages

**Fix first**
1. **Do not copy Francis's review tactic.** Staged $5 reviews are a suspension risk, and clients now spot them.
2. **Trim the padding.** Remove the 42 Claude Code employment entries and the 5 institute education entries. Keep Roblox employment and other experiences to the ones you can discuss in an interview. Padded employment is common among the zero-earning profiles in this sample, not the earning ones.

**Build proof**
3. **Real proof in the portfolio**: links to published Roblox places you built, visits or CCU if you have them, and 30–60 second video clips of real systems (Jay V. wins on this alone). I can turn your raw clips into polished videos.
4. **One proof line at the top of the overview** with real numbers once you have them (games shipped, visits, players).

**Open low-risk doors**
5. **Turn on consultations** (Upwork's "Book a consultation", $20–35 for 30 min). Abdullah turned a $35 call into two $3K contracts. Send written notes after each call.
6. **Publish the 5 Project Catalog listings.** Adejuwon and Ilesanmi got Roblox orders without sending proposals.
7. **Add a tutoring offer.** Roblox tutoring paid Oma ≈$4K, Andrew ≈$7.3K, Dexter $30K+. Add a catalog listing ("1-on-1 Roblox Studio & Luau lessons") and a line in the overview.
8. **Use your real differentiator: Roblox + web/API/AI.** Avirup's urgent TTS job became a retainer. Pitch Open Cloud, webhooks, AI NPCs and fixing AI-built games.

**How to apply**
9. **Apply early to small, urgent, low-competition jobs** (under 5 proposals, $30–300, "fix", "urgent", "trial") and aim to turn them into ongoing hourly work. Rumin, Avirup and Niniola all grew this way.
10. **Chase trend genres** (Brainrot, Steal-a, Grow-a-Garden, 99 Nights style), but scope them honestly.
11. **Never take a full game for $100–400 fixed.** Every 1★ in this sample came from over-promised builds or missed timelines.
12. **Over-communicate**: daily or every-other-day updates with clips, clarifying questions before you start, and early warnings about problems (Pavlo's "you'll hear about it early").
13. **Rate**: start at $15–20/hr, raise to $25+ after 5–10 Roblox reviews.

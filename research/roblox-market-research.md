### 3. Policy / platform changes affecting devs
- **Age checks** [V] Roblox requires facial age estimation (Persona) or ID check to access chat — select markets Dec 2025, worldwide Jan 2026; users bucketed into age bands (<9, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18–20, 21+); age checks extend to Studio collaboration features. — https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat , https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/roblox-requires-users-worldwide-age-check-access-chat-2026-01-07
- **Kids/Select accounts** [S] Roblox Kids (5–8) and Roblox Select (9–15) accounts rolled out from June 2026. Creators wanting their games in those catalogs need ID (or parent) verification, 2-step verification, and active Roblox Plus ($4.99/mo; launched Apr 30, 2026, replacing Premium). Social hangouts, free-form drawing, sensitive-issue games excluded by default. — https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/roblox-introduces-new-age-based-accounts-and-expanded-parental-controls-users-under , https://youthsportsbusinessreport.com/roblox-rolls-out-age-based-accounts-and-expanded-parental-controls-for-users-under-16/
- **Content maturity labels** [V] Labels: Minimal, Mild, Moderate, Restricted (Restricted = age-verified 18+ only). Experiences without complete maturity info have restricted playability; unrated experiences limited to devs/collaborators until the Maturity & Compliance Questionnaire is done. — https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity , https://techcrunch.com/?p=2912012
- **Paid random items** [V] Must disclose all outcomes with numerical odds as percentages summing to exactly 100%, before purchase; applies to items bought with Robux directly or via paid in-game currency, incl. luck boosts/pity systems and indirect cases (e.g., buy seed → random result). Pop-up trigger needs a descriptive label (a bare "(i)" icon is insufficient). Trading of paid-random results restricted for ineligible users per API. — https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items
- **AI tooling** [V] Studio has a built-in MCP server letting AI clients (Claude, Cursor, etc.) explore the DataModel, write scripts, run Luau, and playtest; can connect to multiple Studio instances. [S] Feb 2026 update added agentic tools (get_console_output, start_stop_play, run_script_in_play_mode, get_studio_mode) and Assistant "Bring Your Own Key" for Claude/OpenAI/Gemini; further MCP update Aug 2026. — https://create.roblox.com/docs/studio/mcp , https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-open-source-studio-mcp-server/3649365 , https://www.moguravr.com/roblox-studio-mcp-server-agentic-update/
- **Luau** [V] New Luau type solver has been rolling out via release notes (beta → features going "Live"); no confirmed GA date found. — https://create.roblox.com/docs/release-notes/release-notes-675 , https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-type-solver-beta/3155804/226 , https://github.com/luau-lang/luau/releases
- **Open Cloud** [V] Roblox recommends API-key/OAuth 2.0 Open Cloud endpoints (stable, regularly updated); legacy cookie-auth APIs may break without notice. — https://create.roblox.com/docs/cloud

### 6. Fake reviews / misrepresentation; Project Catalog
- [V] "Feedback building" (fake feedback for own/others' profile, fake contracts, hiring yourself, paying/refunding or free work for reviews, threatening bad feedback) violates ToS → account suspension; violating feedback removed. — https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/17989850384531--What-is-feedback-building
- [V] Represent yourself authentically; one account/profile; no account sharing/transfer. — https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/9127142196243-How-to-represent-yourself-authentically-on-Upwork , https://www.upwork.com/resources/leading-voices/getting-banned-on-upwork
- [S] ToU prohibits "misrepresenting your experience, skills, or information" (quoted by community; check live ToU). [I] Showing others' games as your own portfolio would fall under this; only list games you actually worked on and specify your role.
- [V] Project Catalog: every project reviewed; rejected for poor images, typos, sharing contact info pre-contract; title "You will get…" (≤75 chars), URL fixed from first title; category determines placement; write targeted, SEO-aware descriptions. — https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058122033 , https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-to-create-project-catalog-service , https://www.upwork.com/resources/catalog-product-description

# How Top Short-Form Editors Write Their Employment History on Upwork

**For:** Harry, short-form video editor in Lagos (title now: "Short-Form Video Editor | Reels, TikTok & YouTube Shorts")
**Data pulled:** 27 October 2026, live from Upwork. **40 freelancers:** 16 Nigerians, 12 from other countries, 12 with no Top Rated badge.
**Paid (boosted) rows:** none. Every ranking below is organic.

---

## 1. Short answer

- **Employment history does not seem to push people up the search.** The two best Nigerian rankers (Emmanuel E., avg rank 1.3; Isaac T., 1.7) have 5 and 1 entries. The three Nigerians with 100 entries had the worst average rank of the group (about 17).
- **The most common style is simple:** a real small company or client (or "Freelance", "Self-Employed", "Upwork") plus a plain job title like "Video Editor".
- **Some put search words into the job title** (for example "Short Form Video Editor", "Reels, Tiktok & Shorts Editor"), and a few even put them in the company name. This is not needed to rank.
- **The profile title does the heavy lifting.** Almost everyone uses: `[Role] | [TikTok, Reels, Shorts] & [one niche]`.
- **The overview follows one pattern:** hook → who I help → proof → services list → tools → call to action (often a free sample) → short keyword list.
- **What seems to lift rank most (our reading, not an Upwork fact):** exact buyer phrases in the title and overview, many recent small contracts, and a high Job Success Score.
- **Fake big brands, 100 invented jobs and fake schools did not buy top ranks.** Avoid them.

---

## 2. Two limits you should know first

**(a) What the data tools can see.** For employment, the Upwork data tools return only the **company name and the job title**. They do **not** return dates or descriptions. They return **no "Other experience" section at all**. So the employment lists below are complete for company and title, but we cannot show what people wrote in their descriptions.

**(b) How "ranking" was measured.** Uma (Upwork's AI) ranks freelancers for clients, but that ranking is only visible to a client on their own job post. We cannot see it. So we used the closest thing we can measure: **how many searches each person appears in, and their average position** in those searches.

- Nigerians: 8 searches filtered to Nigeria (short form, TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts, UGC, CapCut, podcast clips, video editor), top 25 each.
- Other countries and no-badge: 7 open searches (no country filter) plus 4 "Rising Talent" searches, top 25 each.

"(our reading)" in this report means it is our interpretation, not a fact from Upwork.

### 4.3 Profile title formula

**`[Role] | [TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts] & [one niche]`**

- 15 of 16 Nigerians use the pipe "|".
- The people found in the most searches name YouTube, Shorts, Reels, TikTok (and often ads/UGC) in the title.
- Common niches: sports, talking-head, podcast clips, wellness, ads/UGC.
- Examples: "Short-Form Video Editor | Talking Head Reels, TikTok & Podcast Clips" (Favour I.); "Short Form Video Editor | YouTube Shorts, Reels & TikTok Editor" (Tatheer A.).

### 4.4 Overview structure (most common order)

1. **Hook** — a question ("Need…?", "Tired of…?") or a bold claim.
2. **Who I help + what I turn into what** — with the platform words (20 of 24 global profiles name Reels/TikTok/Shorts in the first 400 characters).
3. **Proof** — numbers (views, videos, earnings) or a named client or review.
4. **Services list** — 4–15 bullets, each reads like a search phrase.
5. **How I work / why me / tools** — CapCut, Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci.
6. **Call to action** — often a free sample, trial edit or audit.
7. **Keyword block** (optional) at the end.

## 5. What to copy and what to avoid

**Copy:**
- Keep platform words in your title (you already do). Add one niche at the end.
- Put the exact buyer phrases (Short-Form Video Editor, TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts) in your first two overview lines.
- Write service bullets that read like searches: "YouTube Shorts editing", "Podcast clips", "Talking-head Reels".
- End with a short keyword line (Paula E. or Tatheer A. style).
- Offer a free sample or trial edit in your call to action.
- Keep employment to 1–4 honest entries. "Freelance" or "Self-Employed" is fine.
- Name portfolio items like searches: "[Format] Edit | [Niche]".

**Avoid:**
- ⚠ Big brands you never worked for (Tencent, S&P Global, Kia Motors, THG).
- ⚠ Invented companies or 100 copy-paste entries — they ranked worst.
- ⚠ Fake schools or impossible degrees.
- ⚠ Claims your stats don't show ("100% JSS", "Top Rated").
- ⚠ Copying job-post text or other freelancers' portfolio text.
- ⚠ Huge repeated or hidden keyword dumps.
- Typos in company names or titles ("reelance", "editer", "Remotly").

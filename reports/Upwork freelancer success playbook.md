# Win first Roblox contracts on Upwork

**Bottom line:** in October 2026 Upwork is a shrinking but pricier marketplace. Q2 2026 active clients fell **4% to 763K**, while spend per client rose **5% to $5,230** and the take rate climbed to **19.8%**. Upwork also cut its 2026 revenue guidance to **$730–750M**, blaming faster AI automation and a weak labor market. For a freelancer, that means fewer buyers, more bidders and fees that keep drifting up. Matching now runs through **Uma**, an AI agent that has shortlisted candidates for clients on every plan since May 2026. Uma reads the job post against the freelancer's profile, so profile wording that matches the job matters more than it used to. Evidence on how a newcomer breaks in is thin, but it points one way. Two field studies (one a randomized experiment) find that a new worker's **first hired-and-reviewed job** raises later hiring and wages. A 2025 study finds that **bidding below the client's budget raises a novice's visibility and hire rate**, and the gains go mostly to stronger workers. Harry is a Lagos Roblox developer at **$18/hr** with **0 jobs** and **about 80 Connects**. For him, the playbook is to fix how Upwork classifies the profile (it still recommends him AI-automation jobs), build one public demo, and send **1–2 highly specific proposals a day** to small, fresh, payment-verified Roblox jobs, priced as fixed-price bids below budget. He should then turn each first client into repeat work while protecting his account. Realistically he should expect **one or two hires from his first ~30 proposals**, not a flood. The ranking jump comes only after the first reviews.

**How this report was sourced:** the research notes behind this report could not open upwork.com, support.upwork.com, community.upwork.com, investors.upwork.com or sec.gov directly. Nearly every Upwork fact, company figure and blog claim therefore comes from **search-engine snippets of the cited pages, not full reads**. Many third-party numbers come from vendors selling proposal tools (GigRadar, UpHunt, AIProposer, GigUp, Snipework) or fintechs selling withdrawal services (Cleva, Raenest). The report labels those as vendor figures. Statements marked **Inference** are analysis, not something a source states. Harry-specific data comes from this project's own local work: an Upwork API account audit and ranking check (October 20, 2026), a pull of the 40 most recent Roblox jobs (October 5–20, 2026), and a 50-profile study of Roblox freelancers.

## Uma and keyword fit now decide who gets seen

Upwork does not publish how it ranks freelancers. An Upwork community moderator says the Best Match job feed uses "a combination of factors," most of them "aligned with the requirements selected and mentioned by the client" ([Upwork Community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Freelancers/Best-Matched-Algorithm-for-quot-Recently-posted-quot-Jobs/m-p/1521926)). The help center says matching "relies heavily" on profile information such as skills, categories and services ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/34954643219987-Intro-to-Upwork)). Talent search results show clients each freelancer's JSS, total earnings and Top Rated status next to the name ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-upwork-search-feature-helps-clients-freelancers-quickly-connect)). The big structural change is **Uma Recruiter**, launched in **October 2025** ([Upwork engineering blog](https://www.upwork.com/blog/uma-recruiter-how-we-built-an-agentic-solution-to-talent-matching-and-hiring)). Since the **May 5, 2026** Spring update it "automatically identifies and surfaces the most relevant professionals" for clients on the free Basic plan too, delivering shortlists in about **six hours**. Upwork says jobs filled within seven days rose **10%** between November 2025 and March 2026 ([Upwork investor release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-updates-spring-2026-ai-powered-innovations-help-small)). Upwork also says Uma powers a majority of new client job posts and runs interviews inside the proposal process ([Upwork investor release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-evolves-uma-ai-ai-work-agent-advances-human-ai)). Vendors claim Uma can invite freelancers on a client's behalf, and that some clients require an Uma "instant interview" before anyone can apply ([Snipework](https://snipework.com/blog/upwork-uma-recruiter-guide); [UpHunt](https://uphunt.io/blog/upwork-uma-recruiter-ai-shortlist-freelancer-guide-2026)). **No Upwork source found confirms either claim.**

**Inference:** invites now come from three channels: a client's manual search, Uma's automatic shortlist, and paid **Boosted Profile** placement, which puts a profile "among the top" when clients are prompted to invite ([Upwork ads guide](https://www.upwork.com/guides/ads)). A 0-job profile cannot compete on earnings or JSS. Its realistic levers are a close text match to the job, a complete profile, recent activity and current availability. Harry's own data shows the gap. On October 20 the profile was **missing from the top 300 for "Roblox developer"** and sat around **#145–148** for "Luau", "Roblox scripter" and the Roblox Studio skill filter. Upwork's recommender returned **only AI-automation jobs (10 of 10)**, because the overview and five of the twenty skills still advertised n8n and Claude automation (local account audit and job pull, October 20, 2026). The new title, "Roblox Developer | Luau Scripter | Game UI, Shops & Monetization", fixes the keyword gap. The automation skills and section must go too, or both Uma and the recommender will keep reading him as an automation freelancer.

The profile itself has changed. Upwork **removed Specialized Profiles on May 28, 2026**: portfolio items and work history moved to the main profile, but specialized titles, overviews and skills were lost. Upwork says rankings were unaffected ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013750068-Create-a-Specialized-Profile)). Earlier project notes recommended a Roblox "specialized profile"; that option no longer exists. The niche has to live in the single main title, overview, skills and category. A complete profile requires a photo, an overview, at least one employment entry and at least one skill. Portfolio items should show the tools used, contain no contact details and use roughly 1000×750 px images. A video intro "builds rapport" ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063208-Sample-Profiles-and-Best-Practices)). **Project Catalog** still lets clients buy fixed packages without a proposal, with up to **20 listings** per freelancer ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360057397533)). Forum anecdotes say new sellers rarely make sales there and attract scammers ([Upwork Community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Freelancers/Project-Catalog-Sales-Generate-Expert-needs/m-p/1266781)). That fits the project's profile study, where Catalog orders were a small extra for two Nigerian Roblox freelancers ($30–$250 orders), not their main channel. The research found **no current 2026 Upwork documentation for paid consultations**. The earlier advice to switch on a $20–35 consultation rests on one freelancer's example (a $35 call that led to two $3K contracts) and on the setting appearing on peer profiles, not on documented conversion data.

### Badges: Rising Talent is the only one in reach

Badges matter because clients see them next to a proposal. Upwork's current help-center wording bases **Rising Talent** on four things: a 100% complete profile showing strong pre-Upwork experience, "positive performance" such as high-quality proposals and on-time delivery, availability kept up to date, and activity in the past 90 days. It is listed with **"No JSS"** ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049702614); [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/resources/talent-badges-explained)). A different version, an Upwork invitation or a **≥4.8 rating plus ≥$250 earned in 12 months**, also circulates ([UpHunt](https://uphunt.io/blog/upwork-rising-talent-badge-2026)). The notes could not establish which version is current. Top Rated sources also conflict on the earnings bar: one Upwork snippet says ">$10,000 in 12 months" ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/resources/talent-badges-explained)) and an older help article says ≥$1,000. Top Rated Plus adds a history of large contracts ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063568-Top-Rated-and-Rising-Talent)). **Expert-Vetted** is invitation-only for roughly the top 1%. The **Job Success Score** reflects contract outcomes and client feedback, is updated daily over **6-, 12- and 24-month windows**, and appears only after enough contract history. Upwork calls **90%+ "excellent"** and says scores **below 79%** make winning work hard ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/38437362753171-What-is-a-Job-Success-Score); [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-to-improve-job-success-score)). Forum users report that **private feedback**, given when a contract closes, can drag JSS down even when the public stars look fine ([Upwork Community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Freelancers/Private-Feedback-s-Effect-on-JSS-and-the-Negative-Non/m-p/658016)). **Inference:** Harry's first two or three contracts will set his first JSS. They should be small, clearly scoped and certain to finish on time.

## Every proposal costs real money, and fees keep moving

**Connects** cost **$0.15** each and pay for proposals and ads ([Upwork Connects calculator](https://www.upwork.com/tools/connects-calculator)). The Basic plan includes **10 free Connects a month**. **Freelancer Plus** costs **$19.99/month** and includes **100 Connects**, credited on the 1st ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-freelancer-plus-worth-it); [Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062888)). The Connects cost of a job "varies per job and can change during the time the job is posted" ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects)). Vendor samples from 2026 put a typical proposal at around **11–12 Connects (range 4–16)** ([useoutbid](https://useoutbid.com/blog/upwork-connects-cost-per-proposal-2026); [Zenlance](https://zenlance.net/upwork-connects/)). Bought Connects reportedly expire after a year and are refunded if a job is cancelled without a hire ([goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026)). **Boosted proposals** are an auction for up to **four** pinned slots. The bid is charged only if the client interacts with the boosted proposal while it is promoted ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406395531795-Boost-your-proposal)). Forum users report bids of **100–700 Connects** on competitive jobs ([Upwork Community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Feedback/Marketplace-Updates-Boosted-Proposals-and-Connects/m-p/1271875)).

**Inference:** 80 Connects buys roughly **6–8 proposals**, so Harry has about a week's budget at 1–2 a day. On price alone, buying Connects outright (100 for $15) beats Freelancer Plus (100 for $19.99). Plus only makes sense for its other features, which the notes could not confirm. Boosting is a poor use of a newcomer's Connects except on a rare, perfect-fit job where the bid table is cheap. The free 10 a month cover about one proposal.

Since **May 1, 2025** the freelancer service fee has been a **variable 0–15% of all earnings, including bonuses**, shown before the contract starts and locked for it ([Upwork fee calculator](https://wf-seo.upwork.com/tools/upwork-fee-calculator); [goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026)). Third parties put typical effective rates at about **10–13%** ([GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-market-report-2026)). Management told investors it expects the take rate to keep rising "driven by dynamic pricing on freelancer fees" ([Yahoo Finance Q2 call summary](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/upwork-inc-q2-2026-earnings-123000363.html)). For Nigeria, fintech sources report **Direct to Local Bank at $0.99** per withdrawal and a new **$2.99 fee on Direct-to-U.S.-Bank** withdrawals for non-U.S. tax addresses from **September 1, 2026**. Payoneer adds up to about 3% FX, and PayPal cannot receive in Nigeria ([Cleva](https://www.getcleva.com/blog/upworks-new-2-99-withdrawal-fee-dont-let-it-cost-you-twice); [Raenest](https://www.raenest.com/blog/upwork-fees-for-african-freelancers)). Both fintechs sell competing products, and the $2.99 fee is not confirmed on Upwork's own site. **Inference:** on a $100 first job, expect roughly $85–88 net after fees, before withdrawal and FX costs. Batch withdrawals to $300+ so fixed fees stay small.

### Account rules where one mistake ends the business

Upwork's Scams FAQ is explicit: it cannot track or recover payments made outside the platform, and freelancers should decline and report any off-platform payment request ([Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/34385274054547-Scams-Frequently-Asked-Questions)). Common scam patterns include moving to Telegram or WhatsApp before a contract, demands to pay for "training" or Connects, overpayment-and-refund tricks, early ID or voice "verification" requests, and malware disguised as contract files ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/resources/spotting-fake-job-posts); [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-scams)). Third-party ToS guides describe a **24-month non-circumvention** window for relationships that start on Upwork, and say merely discussing off-platform payment in Messages is a violation ([Freelance Filter](https://www.freelancefilter.com/upwork-freelancer-terms-of-service-2026)). Detection reportedly uses device fingerprinting and IP checks to find duplicate accounts, and names must match across the ID, the tax form and the payout method ([GoLogin](https://gologin.com/blog/upwork-account-suspended/); [goLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-account-suspended-what-to-do-next)). Identity crackdowns do happen at scale: Ukrainian freelancers were mass-suspended after ID checks in July 2025 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upwork)). Nigerian anecdotes report suspensions after verifying with a NIN slip, and advise an international passport whose name matches the profile exactly ([LinkedIn](https://de.linkedin.com/in/zsolt-farnas)). Upwork does not confirm this. **Inference:** Roblox clients live on Discord, and the Roblox ecosystem has a live malware campaign that hijacks developers' games through fake job offers. Harry's rules should be: keep every pre-contract message on Upwork; never run files a prospect sends; never use a VPN or share the device; work only in a client-owned or duplicate test place via Team Create; never ask for or accept a Roblox password.

## A smaller, pricier, AI-saturated market rewards specialists with proof

Upwork's numbers describe consolidation. Full-year 2025 revenue was a record **$787.8M**, and **AI-related GSV topped $300M annualized, up more than 50%** ([Upwork Q4 2025 release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial)). By Q2 2026, GSV was **$966.4M (−4%)**, active clients **763K (−4%)**, revenue **$191.7M (−2%)**, and GSV per active client **$5,230 (+5%)**, the eighth straight quarter of growth on that last measure ([Upwork Q2 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001627475/000162747526000046/upwork2q26-pressrelease.htm)). Management cut 2026 revenue guidance to **$730–750M from $760–790M**, assuming "a heightened pace of AI-related automation and no improvements in the labor market" plus weaker Google referrals; the shares fell about 20% after hours ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/upwork-q2-2026-ai-disruption-guidance-cut)). In May 2026 Upwork cut about **24% of its workforce** ([Upwork 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001627475/000162747526000047/upwk-20260630.htm)). Analysts tied the rising take rate partly to "increased competition for jobs" ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/upwork-inc-q2-2026-earnings-123000363.html)). AI-related GSV growth slowed to about **22%** ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/upwork-q2-2026-ai-disruption-guidance-cut)). Strategy is moving upmarket: Business Plus for SMBs grew GSV **24% quarter over quarter** in Q4 2025 ([Upwork Q4 2025 release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial)), and the Lifted enterprise subsidiary launched in August 2025. Q3 2026 results had not been found as of October 24; Upwork usually reports in early November. **Inference:** a typical freelancer now faces fewer buyers, more competitors per job and a higher fee share. The platform's growth sits with larger, AI-adjacent and repeat-client work, not cheap one-off gigs.

AI is reshaping demand in both directions. Upwork's In-Demand Skills 2026 report shows top AI skills up **109%**, with AI integration **+178%** and AI chatbot development **+71%**, while core skills such as full-stack development held steady ([Upwork press release](https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upworks-in-demand-skills-2026-demand-for-top-ai-skills-more-than-doubles-as-ai-is-embedded-into-everyday-work)). Upwork research says freelancers doing AI work earn **34% more per hour**, while per-contract earnings in lower-complexity creative work fell **13%** ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/06/30/ai-job-vibe-coding-upwork)). On the client side, proposals are flooded with near-identical AI text. Vendor GigRadar claims 80–90% of proposals share stock AI phrasing ([GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/ai-proposals-upwork)), and a hirer quoted by Forbes described being "inundated" with bland proposals ([Forbes Australia](https://www.forbes.com.au/news/innovation/chatgpt-writing-how-ai-is-upending-the-freelance-world/)). Third-party summaries say AI assistance is allowed but mass automated submission is not ([GetMany](https://getmany.com/blog/upwork-ai-policy)). In Harry's own October job pull, Roblox clients wrote **"No AI-generated work"** and asked applicants to "explain exactly what you personally handled" (local job pull).

### Roblox demand is real but small and price-sensitive on Upwork

Game development does not appear among Upwork's headline growth skills, and no Upwork data on Roblox posting volume was found. Off Upwork, the money is growing. Roblox DevEx paid creators about **$1B in 2025**, up from $923M in 2024 ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Roblox-continues-to-reward-game-content-creators-as-payouts-cross-1-billion.1157555.0.html)). Mellow, a contractor-payments vendor, reports studio spending on game-dev freelancers up **55%**, with one- to three-month contracts growing fastest, yet **54%** of freelancers say suitable work is hard to find ([Mellow](https://mellow.io/news/mellow-research-freelancing-in-game-development)). One guide puts the median Upwork game-dev rate at about **$28/hr**, with most postings at **$15–35/hr** ([Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-guides/freelance-game-development-how-to-find-clients-and-set-rates)).

### Alternatives complement Upwork rather than replace it

Fiverr is moving the same way, but faster: **2.7M active buyers (−21.9%)**, spend per buyer **$368 (+15.6%)** and revenue **−10%** in Q2 2026, with roughly a **28% take rate** ([Fiverr Q2 2026](https://www.fiverr.com/news/fiverr-q2-2026-earnings)). Contra charges freelancers no commission but centers on design and video ([Contra](https://contra.com/commission-free)). Roblox-native hiring happens on Talent Hub, the DevForum and Discord servers such as HiddenDevs and RoDevs ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-start-making-commissions/1249757)). Those channels often pay in Robux or revenue share and lack escrow. **Inference:** Upwork's value for Harry is **USD escrow and access to non-native buyers** such as YouTubers, brands and first-time founders. Use the DevForum and Discord for portfolio visibility and community credibility, not as the main income channel until he has a reputation.

## Evidence favors cheap first bids, specific openings and fast follow-through

The strongest evidence about cold starts is academic. In **Pallais's 2014 oDesk field experiment**, 952 of 3,767 novice applicants were randomly hired for 10-hour jobs and given public evaluations. Being hired, and especially receiving a *detailed* evaluation, "substantially improved later employment outcomes": treated workers later earned more and asked for higher wages ([NBER w18917](https://www.nber.org/papers/w18917)). A 2025 study of a large global freelancing platform describes a **"reputation trap"**. **44% of 481 novices** believed bidding below the posted budget signals low quality. In fact, lower bids raised both application visibility and the chance of being contacted, and the gains went mainly to *stronger* workers ([IGC](https://www.theigc.org/publications/cutting-wages-break-how-information-gaps-hold-back-workers-online-gig-economy); [World Bank blog](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/impactevaluations/lower-prices--lower-chances--how-misbeliefs-keep-freelancers-out)). That paper is preliminary, and the exact effect sizes were not visible in the snippets. Neither study shows that a permanently low *profile* rate helps. One unverified blog claim says very low rates hide profiles from experienced clients. **Inference:** keep the public $18/hr. Discount the *bid* on small fixed-price first jobs, and raise the rate after the first strong reviews; one vendor guide suggests doing so after the first three ([UpworkAlerts](https://upworkalerts.com/blog/how-to-make-money-on-upwork)).

On proposals, Upwork's own guide says clients first see only the **opening lines**, next to the photo, rate, badges and JSS, and decide from that whether to open the rest ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/guides/resumes-and-cover-letter)). The best large dataset is vendor-owned and correlational: GigRadar's study of **133,872 proposals** (December 2025–February 2026), mostly from agencies. It found a 7.45% baseline reply rate. Closing with an offer to answer questions was associated with **+5.9 points** of reply rate, and offering a one-minute Loom with **+3.7 points** ([GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-proposal-response-rate)). UpHunt says video helps only when it is personalized to the job ([UpHunt](https://uphunt.io/blog/upwork-video-proposals-loom-reply-rates-2026)). Vendor guides converge on these rules: **100–200 words**, open with the client's specific problem rather than "I am a professional with X years", apply within hours, and favor jobs with **fewer than 20 proposals**, avoiding those **older than 72 hours** ([AIProposer](https://aiproposer.com/guides/upwork-proposals/upwork-proposal-win-rate); [GigUp](https://giguphq.com/blog/win-rate-on-upwork-2026)). Their benchmark win rates for newcomers are **2–8% in the first 30 proposals** ([GigUp](https://giguphq.com/blog/win-rate-on-upwork-2026)). Upwork publishes no win-rate data.

On choosing jobs, Upwork's scam guidance supports one hard filter: payment verified. Blogs add client signals such as a hire rate above about 60–70% and meaningful total spend ([Raenest](https://www.raenest.com/es-ar/blog/how-to-get-freelance-jobs-on-upwork)). One 100%-JSS freelancer passes on client accounts under 30 days old unless the job is very well defined ([dev.to](https://dev.to/umair_ulhassan_d200ebd9e/5-signals-framework-how-i-hit-100-job-success-on-upwork-5co7)). Vendor advice says to start with well-scoped fixed-price work rather than the biggest or cheapest jobs ([UpworkAlerts](https://upworkalerts.com/blog/upwork-tips-for-beginners)). Harry's local job pull adds Roblox-specific red flags: pay only on 100% completion, 25-feature MVPs for $100, "clone this named game", likes inflation, "recover stolen pets" and item-delivery bots.

Reviews must be earned honestly. Upwork's 2026 guidance is to do quality work, communicate regularly, anticipate the client's needs and ask for feedback, noting that "even one strong relationship can lead to repeat projects" ([Upwork](https://upwork.com/resources/how-to-become-a-freelancer)). The safe ask is **one short message after delivery asking the client to close the contract and share feedback**, with no request for a particular rating and no repeated nudges ([GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/how-to-ask-upwork-clients-for-feedback)). The project's 50-profile study shows why this matters. Every 1★ review in the sample came from over-promised builds, missed timelines or pressuring for a 5★. A Nigerian profile with staged-looking $5 "full game" reviews had earned only **$10+**. Almost every 5★ review praised communication rather than code, and the no-badge earners grew through small trials that became retainers (a $240 urgent job became a $4.3K retainer; a $1,104 trial was followed by a $2,422 contract) or upsells to the same client (map → map → scripting → obby, about $1,150).

## The 30/60/90-day plan and daily routine for Harry

Everything in this section is **inference** built from the evidence above, Harry's account audit and the Roblox job pull. The plan rests on four rules. First, Upwork must classify the profile as Roblox. Second, one public demo must prove the niche. Third, Connects are spent only where a newcomer can realistically win. Fourth, every first job is chosen so it ends early, on time and with a review.

### Days 1–30: fix the classification, build proof, land the first review

**Days 1–3 (profile).** Keep the title "Roblox Developer | Luau Scripter | Game UI, Shops & Monetization" and the $18/hr rate. Delete the n8n/Claude automation section and the five automation skills, and replace them with Roblox skills: Roblox Studio, Lua/Luau, Game Development, Simulation Game, In-App Purchases/Game UI. Open the overview with one line that matches what clients search for: "I build Roblox systems that save, sell and work on phone and PC: DataStore saving, game-pass shops, rebirth, tycoon/simulator/obby and 'Steal a' mechanics". Keep the safety promise (Team Create, a duplicate test place, never your password) and describe AI as "AI-assisted, human-built and tested". Trim employment to about 8–10 honest entries with consistent locations and filled-in roles. Set availability, add a 60-second video intro, and confirm that the ID name, tax form and payout method match exactly.

**Days 3–10 (proof).** Build one public demo place: an egg-hatch or crate shop with a compliant odds display, three game passes, DataStore saving and rebirth, scaling cleanly on phone and PC. Record three **30–60-second clips**: the shop purchase flow, saving that survives a rejoin, and the mobile layout. Publish each clip as a portfolio item that states what you personally built and which tools you used, with no contact details. Submit 3–5 small Project Catalog items, for example "Game-pass shop + DataStore save" and "Odds-disclosure UI retrofit for egg/crate games". Treat them as low-cost extras.

**Days 1–30 (proposals and Connects).** Send **1–2 proposals a day, about 30–40 in the month**, which costs roughly **350–450 Connects**. The current 80 Connects last about a week. Buy Connects in **100-Connect bundles ($15) as needed**, about **$40–55 for the month**; buying bundles is cheaper per Connect than Freelancer Plus. Do not boost. Target **one or two hires** from these proposals; the vendor benchmark of 2–8% in the first 30 proposals implies one or two.

**Days 1–30 (first contracts).** Accept small fixed-price contracts (roughly **$30–150**) bid at or slightly below the client's budget. Price a $5 "trial" at a real fixed amount, or take it only if it is a genuine one-hour task from a client with hundreds of reviews. Send a progress clip every day or two, deliver early, and ask once for the contract to be closed with feedback.

### Days 31–60: convert first clients and widen the funnel

Turn every finished job into a next-step offer, such as "save system done; want the rebirth multiplier or the shop next?". Keep sending **1–2 proposals a day** (about **350–450 Connects**, about $50–65). Shift toward **$100–300 fixed-price jobs and hourly $15–35 jobs** once you have one or two 5★ reviews. Add a second demo in a different genre, such as a "repair without breaking it" before/after, a tower-defense upgrade shop or a city-RP job/economy system. After three strong reviews, raise the public rate to **$22/hr**. Check search position for "Roblox developer", "Roblox scripter" and "Luau" every two weeks, and check whether the Best Match feed now shows Roblox jobs. If it still shows AI-automation jobs, keep cutting non-Roblox wording. Try one Boosted Proposal only on a perfect-fit job whose bid table shows under about 30 extra Connects. In parallel, post the demos on the DevForum portfolio category for credibility, but keep paid work on Upwork. Target: **3–5 completed contracts**, at least one repeat client, and Rising Talent if the current criteria apply.

### Days 61–90: move from first jobs to repeat and hourly work

Prioritize retaining existing clients over new proposals. Offer an ongoing hourly arrangement, for example "10 hours/week for live-ops fixes and monetization updates", to any client with a live game. Drop to **about one proposal a day**, reserved for jobs above $150 or hourly work. Answer every invite within a few hours. Add a fixed-price **monetization audit** (price ladder, starter pack, odds compliance) as a higher-value entry offer. Optionally test a "Roblox Studio and Luau lessons" listing, since tutoring paid three earners in the profile study $4K–30K+. Raise to **$25/hr** once you have 8–10 reviews or a JSS appears at 90%+. Target: **6–10 completed contracts**, a first JSS, one retainer-style client, and invites arriving without proposals.

## Conclusion

For a new freelancer, Upwork's 2026 decline changes the strategy more than it changes the odds. With fewer clients, higher fees and AI agents doing the first round of matching, both Uma and the human client reward a profile and proposal that read as an exact match to one job. A generalist with a polished bio does not get that reward. Harry's biggest early problem was not his rate or skill. It was that Upwork classified him as an AI-automation freelancer. Fixing that costs nothing, and it matters more than any amount of boosting.

The evidence on cold starts is the most encouraging part of the research. Underpricing the first few *bids* is not a sign of weakness. It is the documented way out of the reputation trap, and it works best for competent people with proof. The biggest remaining unknown is how many of the invites Uma generates reach 0-review profiles at all. Until that is clear, Harry should assume all early visibility comes from his own proposals. He should keep Connects spending as disciplined as a cash budget and treat account safety as worth more than any single job.
